01 · Find professional help

Your GP can help you understand what is happening and discuss suitable care. If you already have a care team, contact them about changes or difficulties.

England

Adults can self-refer to NHS Talking Therapies for anxiety and depression. The service assesses whether it is suitable; local eligibility and waits vary.

Find NHS Talking Therapies

Scotland

Speak to your GP about psychological therapies. Some local services also offer digital self-referral.

Explore NHS Scotland options

Wales

Ask your GP or local health board about counselling and psychological support. Access arrangements vary locally.

Northern Ireland

Speak to your GP about support and referral to an appropriate service in your Health and Social Care Trust.

02 · Put it into your own words

Use these optional notes for yourself or an appointment. Write as much or as little as you like.

These notes are not sent to SHIFT, saved to your account or read by a clinician. They stay on this page until you clear or leave it. Copying or printing creates a separate copy: use a device you trust.

At the appointment, also mention any concerns about your safety, relevant health conditions and medicines you take. You do not need to type those details here.

03 · While you wait — and if things change

I haven’t managed to make contact

Make the next step smaller: write down the number or open the referral page. Ask someone you trust to sit with you while you make contact. Tell the service about communication or access needs.

I’ve contacted a service and I’m waiting

Check they received your request. Ask when to expect a response, who to contact while waiting and what to do if things worsen. If you cannot manage the wait, contact your GP or existing care team.

I have an appointment

Bring your notes. Ask what the next step is, who is responsible for your care and when you will review how it is going.

The help isn’t helping

Tell the professional what has changed, what feels difficult and what you hoped would improve. Ask for a review of the plan and other options. Discuss medicine changes with your prescriber rather than changing or stopping them yourself.

Choose one manageable thing for today. Practical support can sit alongside treatment; it does not replace it.

You do not have to wait for an appointment or a reply if you feel unsafe. Find urgent support.
