# R3 My Timber — iPhone Safari recording gate

## Setup

- Open the isolated `workers.dev` preview URL in iPhone Safari.
- Use a fresh disposable email and a password of at least ten characters.
- Record in portrait with the Safari address bar visible at the start.
- Confirm there is no horizontal movement before beginning.

## Required continuous journey

1. Open **Today** and tap **Life changed?**. The bottom sheet must appear immediately without searching or scrolling.
2. Tap **Working late**. Capture the in-place replacement with **Now**, **Next**, **Later** and **Why this?** visible.
3. Tap **Life changed again?**, then **Sort my next three hours**. Capture Chaos Mode’s new dominant **Now** action.
4. Tap **Fakeaway**. Capture food, movement, hydration and the explanation changing together without a page reload.
5. Tap **That’s today handled**. Capture the **Today shifted** completion message and useful observation.

## Acceptance checks visible in the recording

- No horizontal overflow, clipped copy or off-brand colours.
- No dead button, loading trap, duplicate submission or unexplained blank state.
- Results appear at the point of action; no hunt down the page.
- The dominant next action is obvious and one-thumb reachable.
- No production account, production customer record or live commercial route is used.
