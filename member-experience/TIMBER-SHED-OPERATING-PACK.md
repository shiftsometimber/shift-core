## 2. Proposed member rules and service description

Proposed member-facing wording, subject to Matt's acceptance:

The Timber Shed is a private, adult peer community for sharing everyday experiences of changing habits and managing weight. It is run by SHIFT. Members and moderators speak from experience; the community does not provide diagnosis, prescriptions, personalised treatment or emergency care.

Choose a community name that does not identify you. Do not post contact details, addresses, prescription labels, identifying photographs, private messages or someone else's personal or health information. A community name does not make a post anonymous to SHIFT.

Share what happened to you without telling another person what dose to take, change or stop. Do not buy, sell, lend or swap medicines, promote unsafe weight-loss practices, or encourage self-harm, disordered eating or substance misuse. No harassment, hate, threats, sexual exploitation, scams or commercial solicitation.

Every new post and reply waits for a human moderator. Screening tools can flag material but cannot diagnose, approve a post or ban a member. Posts may be withheld or removed. Use the report control for concerns and block controls to hide interactions. You can request a review of a rejected/removed post or access restriction; a different moderator handles that review.

The Shed is not monitored continuously. Do not wait here for urgent help. If someone may be in immediate danger, use local emergency services. The final help panel must contain checked resources appropriate to the pilot's locations.

You can withdraw your posts, export your community information and leave the community. Leaving clears your chosen name and post text from the active forum, but limited moderation records and restrictions may remain under the published retention policy. Deleting a forum post cannot recall screenshots or material another person has already copied.

Before publication add the actual review hours, routine response target, complaint contact and adopted retention periods. Do not publish placeholders or imply support coverage that has not been staffed.

## 3. Human moderation procedure

### Before each review session

Use an individual authorised moderator account on a secured device. Confirm who owns the session and who can take handover. Check open reports, pending posts, appeals and unresolved incidents. Do not use shared credentials. If cover fails, stop new participation or close the pilot through the approved feature-flag procedure; never rely on unobserved queues.

Review the full post and relevant context. Check personal information, bullying/discrimination, illegal content, medication instructions/trading, eating-disorder encouragement, self-harm and safeguarding concerns. A low screening score does not substitute for reading. Ordinary approval must remain unavailable for high/critical or outdated screening decisions.

### Decisions

| Situation | Action | Record / message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Safe everyday peer experience | Approve after full human review | Audited approval; generic in-app notice |
| Unclear clinical instruction or identifying detail | Keep withheld; reject for safer resubmission where suitable | Controlled reason; do not repeat sensitive text in email |
| High/critical screening or a safeguarding concern | Keep withheld; do not override through a database edit or appeal | Restricted incident record; owner review; external escalation only through a justified, authorised process |
| Credible immediate danger | Do not attempt diagnosis, counselling or promises of rescue | Use emergency/safeguarding procedure appropriate to location; disclose only what is necessary and lawful; record factual rationale |
| Report on existing material | Read context, hide/remove if justified; assess connected replies | Audit action and factual reason; protect reporter identity |
| Repeated harmful conduct | Human evidence review; proportionate suspension or ban | Controlled reason, duration, confirmation and audit; never automatic |
| Appeal | Allocate to an active moderator who did not make the decision and is not the author | Uphold or return post to pending; access appeal may reinstate; never bypass high-risk holds |
| Complaint about Matt / another moderator | Preserve evidence and route to an independent authorised person | Current software does not supply an administrator sanction pathway for active moderators; do not claim it does |

If no suitably qualified reviewer is available, keep clinical/specialist-risk content withheld and offer only the approved scope message. AI is not a replacement for a clinician. A disclaimer does not make unsafe treatment advice acceptable.

### Proposed response text (manual use only)

Routine withholding: "We have kept this post out of the community because it includes advice or details outside the Shed's peer-support rules. You can resubmit an account of your own experience without medication instructions or identifying details."

Review outcome: "A different moderator has reviewed this decision. [It has been upheld / your post has returned to the moderation queue / your community access has been reinstated]. Returning a post to the queue does not publish it."

Urgent scope reminder: "The Shed cannot provide emergency support or clinical advice. Please use the appropriate urgent service for your situation and location; do not wait for a community reply."

These are draft manual responses, not approved emails. No message, recipient or sending has been authorised by this pack.

### Incident record and handover

Keep incident evidence in a restricted, approved system, not in general community posts, screenshots on personal devices or ordinary email. Use an incident ID, time, post/account reference, observed facts, actions, reason, decision owner, next review time and escalation outcome. Do not speculate about diagnosis or collect extra medical history. Retention and access must follow the adopted privacy policy.

Handover must identify unresolved incidents and deadlines, remaining holds and who has accepted responsibility. An unread in-app notification is not an accepted handover. Record a conflict of interest and reassign it.

### Training / rehearsal acceptance

Both reviewers must demonstrate: routine approval; missed risk despite low score; unsafe medication advice; identifying data; a credible threat; an age concern; block evasion; suspended-member access; failed-audit rollback; an appeal of their own decision; export/leave and retained records; unavailable cover; a complaint about the owner. Use fictional data only. Record date, participants, result and correction. No rehearsal has been completed by naming Matt as owner.

## 4. Privacy and DPIA preparation

This is an assessment draft, not completed legal clearance. Health-related member disclosures can be special-category data even with a pseudonym. The controller must establish and document an Article 6 lawful basis and a separate Article 9 condition before processing such data. Private membership does not remove those obligations.

### Assessment to complete before real-member activation

Record service purpose, alternatives (including no forum and a smaller nonclinical pilot), information flow, affected people and expected benefit. Assess necessity and proportionality of each field. Document consultation where appropriate and the actual Article 6 / Article 9 choices; do not write "consent" as an automatic answer.

Assess at least these risks: member reidentification; sensitive disclosures; unsafe advice reaching members; staff misuse; inaccurate automated flags; inadequate age checks; failed coverage; account compromise; evidence lost on departure; over-retention; provider access/transfers; quoted content surviving withdrawal; inability to provide an independent appeal. Rate likelihood/severity before and after controls, assign an owner and evidence each mitigation.

The existing premoderation, server-side authorisation, privacy-scoped exports, independent appeals, transaction/audit guards and default-off provider reduce some risks. They do not demonstrate that residual risk is acceptable. Obtain any specialist advice necessary for an unresolved clinical, safeguarding, data-protection or regulatory risk; otherwise keep the relevant capability closed.

## 5. Online safety and children's access worksheets

Record the actual UK service scope and who may encounter user-generated content. A private, small or adult-labelled service is not automatically exempt from Online Safety Act duties.

Children's access: record service design, age controls and evidence. Only conclude that children cannot normally access the service if the official criteria are met; DOB self-declaration and email verification alone do not establish highly effective age assurance. Otherwise complete the required likelihood assessment. Record outcome, reasons, review dates and triggers; apply relevant children's duties if the outcome requires them. Do not select or purchase an age provider without separately assessing necessity, data handling and cost.

Illegal-content risk: work through Ofcom's current guidance/toolkit against actual features, membership, reach, discoverability and likely misuse. Record each applicable harm, likelihood/impact, controls, remaining gaps, accountable owner and review trigger. Include reporting/complaints, access decisions, moderation evidence, staff training and incident handling. Use the published guidance's categories rather than assuming this worksheet is a complete legal risk assessment.

No live chat, direct messaging, media uploads or automated external notifications should be added as an incidental change; each materially changes this assessment. Complete these records for the actual service configuration, not only the static design preview.
