# Shift AI test voice

Matt approved the voice direction in the v2 build pack. These implementation strings are proposed coaching copy; independent voice and relevance review is still BLOCKED. They are not quotations from his book.

Use plain British English. One useful action, with a truthful reason. Be warm, direct and practical. No shaming, hype, nagging, forced “mate”, clinical claims or invented personal experience. Humour is optional; omit it around a health concern. Attribute an observation to its record. Never invent a win.

Examples in this build:

- Just starting: “Start with one thing you can actually use.”
- Already making changes: “Keep what works. Change what gets in the way.”
- Hit a wall: “A hard week counts too. Keep this small.”
- Return: “Good to have you back. Start with today.”
- Not tried: “Do you still want this action?”
- Helpful outcome: “You marked your last action as done and helpful.”
- Wrong context: display the member’s corrected fact, never explain away the correction.
- Concern: the fixed help panel explains that nobody monitors the chat. No generated health advice.

The action library in `voice.mjs` uses the public Life Back page as a reference for everyday planning. `test_library` is not a clinical approval status. Source publication never grants blanket coaching approval. Before real use, record approval of the exact passages and their versions, review all derived strings and withdraw dependants when their sources change.

Review checklist: does the action fit the stated week; is the reason traceable; did the next step change after feedback; is the member’s own voice respected; is any claim unsupported? Any shaming, hype, nagging or pretend-clinician language is a FAIL.
