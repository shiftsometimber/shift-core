A CLEARER PICTURE

Your score, explained.

You rate six areas of life from 0 to 100, reflecting on how you feel right now. Every area counts equally.

0 means “far from where I want to be”. 100 means “where I want to be”. This is your personal reflection, not a medical assessment.

What does the change mean?

The change compares your average with your previous completed check-in for the same goal. We calculate it before rounding; the dates are always shown.

If you skip an area, your answers stay as a draft. Missing answers never count as zero. Choosing a new personal goal starts a fresh set of ratings. Your old entries remain in your history.
