## Full description

My Timber is the private member space from SHIFT Some Timber.

Use Today as your starting point, keep useful health and progress information in
one place, and move through practical food, movement and programme support
without turning every day into a project.

Inside My Timber you can:
- check in and keep track of progress;
- see your Next Shift — one useful next step;
- use SHIFT Grub and SHIFT Fit member tools;
- manage supported account and member details;
- review supported orders and programme information;
- request account deletion from Settings.

My Timber provides programme support and general health information. It does not
replace a doctor, diagnose a condition or independently prescribe prescription
medicine. Where a regulated clinical service is involved, the relevant clinical
or pharmacy provider is responsible for its own assessment and regulated care.

Existing My Timber members use their existing SHIFT account.

## Reviewer notes

My Timber is the authenticated member area of SHIFT Some Timber. The app opens
into Today and uses the same account and server-side member records as the web
service. It is not a separate medical-record system and does not itself prescribe.

Reviewer access must use a dedicated fixture account containing no real member
health information. Supply the reviewer username/password only through the store's
secure reviewer fields, never source control or PR comments.

Suggested review path:
1. Sign in.
2. Open Today.
3. Complete a fixture check-in and confirm Next Shift.
4. Open Grub and Fit.
5. Open Settings / Member details.
6. Locate Request account deletion (do not submit unless the fixture is disposable).
7. Sign out and test forgot-password only with the dedicated fixture mailbox.

If any paid/clinical route is reachable in the exact candidate, reviewer notes
must describe the hand-off and payment responsibility accurately after final
commerce acceptance. Do not promise a treatment or stock state in these notes.

## v1 notification scope

The store app v1 does **not** claim native check-in reminders. The PWA reminder
service remains unchanged. The native app hides the browser/PWA install and
reminder controls rather than presenting a control that cannot be honoured by
APNs/FCM. Native reminders are a post-v1 feature unless APNs/FCM is implemented
and physically proven before submission.

This is an explicit v1 scope decision for deployability, not a claim of full
notification parity with the PWA.
