Keep the introduction useful

A clear handover.

  • Share the public link so the person can explore for themselves.
  • Do not send someone else’s health or contact information to SHIFT.
  • Do not imply that signposting guarantees treatment, medicine or a particular result.
  • Clinical responsibility stays with the person’s own clinical service.
  • Discuss organisational arrangements through the referral enquiry page.
PROFESSIONAL / COMMUNITY

Simple signposting.

No referral payment. No transfer of clinical responsibility. The person chooses their own next step.

AFFILIATE / AUDIENCE

Agree the arrangement first.

Non-clinical offers only. Rates, attribution and payment terms need agreement in writing before activity begins.

PAID RELATIONSHIPS

Make the relationship clear.

Use the disclosure agreed for that placement. This pack does not establish an affiliate agreement or authorise a campaign.
