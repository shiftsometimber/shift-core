# Shift AI R4 invite-only pilot

Consent version: `shift-ai-r4-pilot-consent-v1`

## Before you decide

Shift Some Timber Ltd is testing a new My Timber feature with a very small invited group. Joining is optional. Saying no will not affect your account, membership or access to the normal Shift service.

The pilot lasts for no more than 14 days. You can stop taking part at any time without giving a reason.

## What the pilot does

If real life changes your day, Shift can:

- read the information already held in your My Timber account;
- select suitable food and movement from your current Shift Grub and Shift Fit plans;
- show you a proposed change to today;
- explain what the change is designed to support; and
- update Today only after you explicitly confirm the exact change.

The pilot does not diagnose, prescribe, change treatment, decide eligibility or replace professional medical advice. If something sounds clinical or urgent, the rebuild should stop rather than attempt to answer it.

## What information is used

The pilot may read information already in My Timber, including your profile, plan stage, targets, preferences, check-ins, progress history, current Grub and Fit plans, recorded constraints and Life Back priorities.

It does not create or edit Grub or Fit catalogue records. It does not persist new AI learning about you. The AI model remains switched off during this pilot.

## What is recorded

Shift records:

- the situation you selected or described;
- the information used to decide whether a rebuild was safe and complete;
- the proposed food and movement identifiers;
- whether you confirmed or left the proposal unchanged;
- any write made after confirmation;
- any safety or incomplete-information stop; and
- technical evidence needed to investigate errors and protect participants.

The pilot team reviews these records and participant feedback daily. Pilot records are handled under Shift Some Timber's privacy information and applicable retention rules. The pilot must not begin until the final privacy notice, retention period and contact details have been approved and supplied to participants.

## Your choices

You do not have to confirm any proposed rebuild. Nothing should be written merely because you opened the feature.

You can withdraw from the pilot at any time using the same contact method used for your invitation or the Shift contact page. Access to the pilot will be revoked. Withdrawal does not undo processing that lawfully occurred before withdrawal; the final approved privacy information must explain whether any audit evidence must still be retained and why.

Contact for withdrawal or questions: **hello@shiftsometimber.co.uk**. A named person must monitor this inbox every pilot day and revoke access promptly when requested.

Privacy notice: **the approved `shift-ai-r4-pilot-privacy-v1` notice must be supplied with this statement before agreement**. Do not rely on a general website privacy link unless it expressly covers this pilot's data use, audit records and retention.

## Explicit agreement

The following statement must be presented separately from general terms, with no pre-ticked box:

> I have read and understood the Shift AI R4 pilot information. I understand what My Timber information the pilot will use, what will be recorded, that the AI model is off, and that nothing changes unless I confirm it. I understand that joining is optional and that I can withdraw at any time. I explicitly agree to take part in this invite-only pilot.

Record the participant's clear affirmative agreement, timestamp, consent version and evidence reference. Do not enable pilot access from silence, continued use or a bundled acceptance.

## Approval gate

This is operational consent wording, not final legal approval. Before use, Shift Some Timber must confirm the appropriate UK GDPR lawful basis and special-category condition, approve the privacy information and retention period, and replace every TBC.

The wording follows the ICO principles that consent must be freely given, specific, informed, unambiguous, evidenced and as easy to withdraw as to give:
