# SHIFT first real-user pilot — ready-to-run protocol

Status: PREPARED, not recruited and not completed. Start with 10–15 adult volunteers after the ordinary public signup route is verified. This is a usability and engagement pilot, not a treatment trial. Stock is not a criterion for participation; nobody needs to buy a medicine or change treatment.

## Invitation draft — not sent

We are testing whether SHIFT is genuinely useful in everyday life, not whether you can work out a website. Try the existing Programme and My Timber for eight weeks and tell us what helps, what is confusing and what you stop using. There is no obligation to buy treatment. Taking part is optional, you can stop at any time, and you should not send medical details or passwords in feedback. Your clinician remains responsible for any treatment decisions. We will agree any use of a named quote separately.

## One ordinary front-door acceptance before recruitment

Use the actual public signup page with the real security check and an accessible test inbox. Confirm receipt of the verification message, successful verification, password login, Start Here answer handoff, consent, first save, logout and a later return. Exercise reset, resend, expired link and replay once in a controlled test. Do not use commissioning access as evidence for this gate. Record a redacted walkthrough. Do not collect live passwords or tokens in the evidence pack.

## Test session

Ask the participant to start from the homepage without instructions on where to click. Observe whether they can find the relevant information, complete Start Here, create and verify an account, find Journey, understand what the Passport does, and save one optional item. They may skip personal health details. Ask them to find one useful meal or movement guide in the existing Grub/Fit tools. Finish by logging out, signing back in and checking the saved item is still present. Record where assistance was required; do not rescue them before recording the obstacle.

## Follow-through

At the end of the first session ask: What did you think SHIFT would help you do? What was useful? What felt unnecessary? What would bring you back? At week one, week four and week eight ask what they used unprompted, what they abandoned and why. Participant contacts and consent stay in the approved operational system, not a public repository or marketing-analytics payload. Screenshots/recordings must exclude real health records and personal identifiers, or use a separate fictional demonstration account.

## Evidence sheet

Use participant codes, not names, in the analysis. Record signup/verification success, time to first useful action, steps requiring help, successful first save, ability to retrieve the record after returning, and the three follow-up dates. Distinguish an invited person from a registered account and a registered account from an activated user. Denominators must exclude people not yet eligible for the week-one/week-four/week-eight window. Separate prompted feedback visits from unprompted use.

## Decision rule

Treat every blocked signup, lost save or cross-account disclosure as a release concern. Fix repeated navigation/comprehension problems before adding features. Report exact counts with sample size and missing follow-ups; do not present percentages from this small volunteer group as proof of health effectiveness. Do not compare pre/post tracking periods as if collection coverage were identical.

Deliverables: a redacted task-completion matrix, the five most frequent barriers, what participants returned for, and a short keep/fix/remove decision log. No real participant data or invented pilot results are included in this preparation.


## Continuity extension — 19 September 2026

Keep the existing 10–15-person usability cohort. Aim for a mix of adults using another provider, people who have already stopped treatment, and people not using medication. Nobody changes treatment for this pilot. Treatment context is optional self-report held in the approved operational record with consent, not inferred from URLs or copied to marketing analytics. With this sample, show exact counts; do not publish identifiable subgroup tables.

Add a day-56 follow-up. The HQ report now includes week eight (days 49–55 after account registration, eligible at 56 complete days). Use `/v1/hq/journey?days=90`; the 30-day default cannot include a mature eight-week cohort. HQ covers all eligible real registrations in that window, not only invited pilot members. Keep the recruited cohort's coded task sheet separate and reconcile membership explicitly before attributing aggregate site changes to this pilot.

Record prompted and unprompted return separately in the pilot sheet; the automatic report cannot distinguish them. It counts authenticated logins or audited Journey/Passport saves, not all check-ins or browsing. Record use of other features through consented participant feedback; do not relabel a login as a useful action.

Post-treatment use is a separate voluntary follow-up for people who had already stopped, with the anchor date recorded only if they choose to supply it. Unknown dates stay unknown. Do not derive a causal treatment outcome from engagement or mark follow-up missing before it is due. Report withdrawal and loss to follow-up alongside the original recruitment count.
