# Independent fictional input author — round 2

Read only this file, catalogue.json and state-template.json in this directory. Do not inspect implementation, tests, prior examples, desired outputs or rule descriptions. Write eight realistic fictional member histories into histories.json. No real people or health records. The recipes are test content only.

Output: an array of {id, title, background, state, memberFollowup}. Every state must be a complete concrete instance of the template, with setup:true, entitlement:{active:true}, revision:0, clock:"2026-09-13", anchor:"2026-08-30", cycle:2. Give each member a fictional first name. Preserve empty technical arrays from the template. preferences must specify allergies ("none", "unknown" or an array of catalogue allergen identifiers), diet ("any", "vegetarian", "unknown"), equipment (e.g. ["hob"]), activityLimitations ("none", "unknown" or the member's stated limitation), plus free text time, household and spending. This records what they state; it does not diagnose.

Saved slots cover two past weeks (31 August and 7 September) and two upcoming weeks (14 and 21 September 2026), with plausible household cooking occasions and chosen walks. It need not be a whole diet. Vary the saved meals, quantities and days across members; use actual recipe IDs in catalogue.json. Each occurrence has a unique id "YYYY-MM-DD:slot-key", date and a stable weekly slotKey such as "wed-dinner" or "sat-walk". Days must match dates. kind:"meal" also needs recipeId, recipeVersion:1, servings (1–12), serveNow (portions eaten at that sitting), completed:false. kind:"move" needs label, minutes, completed:false. Optional kind:"leftovers" needs servings, sourceId referencing its actual cooking occurrence, completed:false; portions eaten plus reserved portions cannot exceed the source's cooked quantity.

Reports refer to actual past saved slots: {slotKey,kind,period,date,shift,status,fit,reason,note}. period is that week's Monday; date is the occurrence date. status is "done", "missed" or "unknown". fit is "manageable", "did-not-fit" or "unknown" (use unknown unless done). shift is the reported pattern; reason/note are their words. Omit unreported actions rather than fabricating results. Maximum one report per weekly slot. Do not report future slots.

requests: explicit member requests {slotKey,date,reason}, linked to a saved slot and dated no later than clock. freezes records a prior decision to leave an item alone: object keyed by slotKey, values {throughCycle:number,evidenceAt:"YYYY-MM-DD"}. Include these only where the fictional narrative actually supplies such a decision; they are not assumptions.

acquired is keyed by shopping period "2026-09-14..2026-09-20" (or the following week), then "ingredientId|unit", then {status:"bought"|"have"|"unknown",quantity:number}. Use catalogue units; unknown quantity is 0. manualItems are {id,name,period} unrelated shopping additions. Keep these realistic where supplied.

memberFollowup records what the fictional member would choose when looking at a review: {preferredMealId: actual catalogue id or null, reason: their words}. This is an input preference, not an expected engine result or permission to substitute an option that was not offered.

Vary ordinary lives: work and household disruption, things going well, uncertainty about why something happened, very little reporting, practical preferences changing, and several concerns at once. Include genuine variability over successive weeks. Do not write expected outputs, rule names or pass/fail expectations. The evaluator's behaviour must not shape these histories. Cases 07 and 08 will be held out from the first evaluation round. Produce full states, not prose awaiting an adapter to invent plans.
