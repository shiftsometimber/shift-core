WHY IT EXISTS

Real life rarely follows the plan.

Work runs late. The kids need you. Dinner changes. Motivation disappears. Shift is designed to give you the next useful move, then adapt when the day moves again.

THE FOUNDER VIEW

Practical support for ordinary blokes.

No perfection theatre. No pretending a medicine fixes the rest of life. Clear information, useful daily support and language that sounds like a human being.

CLINICAL INDEPENDENCE

A clinician makes the clinical decision.

Shift can explain governed treatment routes. It does not promise eligibility or choose a medicine or strength. Any future treatment request requires a full assessment and an independent qualified clinician’s decision.

THE DIFFERENCE

Plans change. Shift changes with you.

My Timber keeps food, movement, hydration and the rest of the week useful when life gets in the way—because getting your life back is bigger than a product.
