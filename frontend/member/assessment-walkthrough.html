Medicine preferences, assessment and refunds

Members may record a medicine or dose preference before consultation. This is a request for assessment, not a prescription or a guarantee of supply. The prescribing clinician considers the request with the member and decides whether treatment is safe and appropriate. They may request further information, recommend an alternative or decline to prescribe.

Members must provide complete and accurate information and tell the clinical team promptly if an answer is incorrect or their health changes. An error may require clarification, further assessment or a pause in the request. It does not automatically result in a refund deduction.

If no prescription is issued, any payment collected for the medicine will be refunded, or an uncaptured authorisation released. A separate assessment or administration charge may be retained only where its exact amount, purpose and applicable refund conditions were clearly disclosed and agreed before payment, the relevant work has actually been provided, and retaining it is lawful and proportionate. No charge may be introduced retrospectively or used as a penalty. Any refund decision must explain the amount returned and the basis of any permitted deduction.

No new assessment or administration fee is introduced by this page. Treatment ordering remains closed until the named providers, charges, payment arrangements and refund process are confirmed. Nothing in these terms limits statutory rights. See cancellations and refunds or raise a payment query.

Every case also needs verified identity and contact details, date of birth, height, weight, GP details and permissions, and appropriate evidence. Images alone are not proof of BMI. The provider must agree how these checks work.
