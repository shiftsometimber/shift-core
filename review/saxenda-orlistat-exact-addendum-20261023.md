## Can you take Saxenda and Orlistat together?

**Do not combine them on your own.** Saxenda (liraglutide) and orlistat work differently, but that does not establish that taking both is safe or more effective. The [UK Saxenda Summary of Product Characteristics](https://www.medicines.org.uk/emc/product/2313/smpc) states that safety and efficacy have not been established in people treated with other products for weight management, and that use in these patients is not recommended.

If you already take either medicine, ask the prescriber responsible for your treatment before adding or switching anything. They need to consider your medical history, other medicines, possible adverse effects and the authorised indications. This page compares the two options; it is not a recommendation to use them together.
