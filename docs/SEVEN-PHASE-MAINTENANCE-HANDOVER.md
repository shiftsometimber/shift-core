# Seven-phase maintenance handover

## Authority and current state

The seven internal phases are accepted complete. The isolated candidate on PR 475 is merge-ready and frozen. It is not production-authorised and medicine purchasing remains unavailable.

The immutable freeze record is `evidence/frozen-candidate-v1.json`. Any maintenance proof must identify its target commit and must not silently move that baseline.

## Phase ownership

| Phase | Accepted internal outcome | Maintenance boundary |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Catalogue foundations and usable HQ intake | Accept external records only through dry-run, bounded apply and rollback; imported data cannot approve itself |
| 2 | Short structured route, including tablet depth | Protect the accepted two-choice path and conditional structured treatment history |
| 3 | Route, catalogue and results form one governed journey | No invented catalogue facts, eligibility conclusion or purchase path |
| 4 | Definitive homepage and governed post-CTA treatment journey | Public surfaces remain service-level; governed surfaces remain item-specific |
| 5 | My Timber rebuild, learning, prediction, weekly insight and morning entry | Regression fixes only; preserve the accepted one-second rebuild behaviour |
| 6 | Governed decision-content operations | Protect version locking, evidence binding, independent approval, withdrawal and audit history |
| 7 | Operational status and release controls | Exact evidence, commit, approval separation, rollback and explicit authorisation remain mandatory |

## Non-negotiable design controls

Only `#050505`, `#E7E3DA` and `#707762` are permitted colour bases. White, off-palette values and same-colour foreground/surface pairings are release failures. Default, selected, disabled, loading, expanded, focus and error states are included in that rule.

## Maintenance rules

- No feature work, redesign, AI work or scope expansion on the frozen candidate.
- No production deployment or production database mutation.
- No weakening of commercial, claims, supplier, stock, purchase or clinical gates.
- A regression repair must be the smallest behavioural restoration possible and must rerun the full candidate suite.
- Documentation, test-only hardening, observability and dependency/security hygiene may proceed when they do not alter product behaviour.

## Minimum regression proof

Before accepting any maintenance change, run the palette and surface-contrast gates, accepted treatment candidate gate, production fail-closed gate, Phase 1–7 tests, iPhone WebKit journey, desktop Chrome/Safari journey and renewed physical iPhone Safari scrutiny when rendered behaviour changes.

Every result must retain its workflow run, artifact digest and target commit. A green test without commit-bound evidence is not release evidence.
