# Shift industrial content objectives

## Scale doctrine
The old 64 Grub / 48 Fit figures are retired as product targets. They remain historical commissioning checkpoints only.

Short-term structured-content objective:
- **2,500 commissioned Grub recipe/food objects.**
- **2,500 commissioned Fit exercise/movement objects.**

Long-term minimum content universe:
- **10,000+ quality-controlled Grub objects.**
- **10,000+ quality-controlled Fit objects.**

The long-term ambition is for Shift to become the practical, structured **Wikipedia of food and movement for ordinary blokes**: enormous trusted universes underneath, tiny personalised choices on top. These are quality objectives, not vanity row counts. Every object must remain useful, meaningfully distinct, structured, provenance-aware and eligible for quarantine/withdrawal when validation fails.

## Grub product constitution
Shift Grub must not become a nutrition spreadsheet with dinner attached. Product inspiration is the breadth, clarity and appetite appeal of **Gousto / HelloFresh / Green Chef**, combined with the normal-food weight-management practicality people recognise from **Slimming World / WeightWatchers**, filtered through a distinctly Shift bloke-friendly voice and experience.

The member-facing test is: **would an ordinary bloke genuinely fancy eating this tonight?** Food should be proper, familiar and useful: breakfasts, work lunches, packed lunches, family dinners, quick meals, slow-cooker and air-fryer food, curries, chilli, pasta, pies, roasts, burgers, kebabs, pizza-style meals, Chinese/Indian-inspired fakeaways, BBQ food, pub-style alternatives and sensible convenience-led options.

Shift must also support **proper treat/cheat/big-feed meals** without moralising. Pizza, curry, Chinese-style food, kebabs, burgers and chips, steak and chips, wings, loaded fries, Sunday roast, pies, mac and cheese, nachos, breakfasts, BBQ and desserts are not forbidden concepts. The intelligence is in portion, frequency, substitutions and the rest of the day/week. No food guilt; no pretending courgette ribbons are chips.

Every recipe remains blocked from production until its exact ingredients, quantities, method, allergens/substitutions, storage/reheating/food-safety where relevant, and ingredient-level nutrition methodology have passed their required gates. Apparent variety from trivial seasoning changes or renamed duplicate recipes is unacceptable.

## Fit product constitution
The Fit universe may reach 10,000+ structured objects without inventing fake human movements. Scale comes from canonical movements plus genuinely useful equipment, machine, bodyweight, band, dumbbell, barbell/cable where appropriate, location, dose, regression, progression, range, support, mobility, cardio, low-impact and limitation-aware variations.

Fit must cover strength, cardio, walking, mobility, stretching, warm-up/cool-down, balance and practical short movement contexts. Anything eligible for member prescription must eventually carry sufficient member-ready guidance and visual QA so Dave does not need to leave Shift to work out what an exercise means.

## Factory KPI
Track both inventory and conversion:

Grub: idea/demand -> authored -> schema-valid -> nutrition/domain-valid -> reviewed -> published -> production-served -> launch-ready -> simulated/monitored.

Fit: idea/demand -> authored -> schema-valid -> domain-valid -> visual-produced -> visual-QA -> reviewed -> published -> production-served -> launch-ready -> simulated/monitored.

At scale, independent batches should occupy multiple stages simultaneously. A failed or uncertain object is quarantined without blocking unrelated clean content. Authoring scale must never be used to hide downstream conversion zeros.

## Current industrial acceleration
The first industrial wave established a 224-object Grub and 224-object Fit quarantined factory batch on top of the original 32+32 structured catalogue. The next scale wave is deliberately approximately 10x: **2,468 industrial Grub drafts + the original 32 = 2,500 authored Grub objects, and 2,468 industrial Fit drafts + the original 32 = 2,500 authored Fit objects.**

The scale-2,500 quality gate requires exact-ID and exact-fingerprint uniqueness, broad meal distribution, substantial bloke-friendly/treat/fakeaway coverage, 102 new canonical Fit mode/movement identities with 22 structured prescription variations each, and quarantine before nutrition/visual/review publication barriers. A counter reaching 2,500 does not make 2,500 items commissioned.

## Operating rule
Industrialise the conveyor belt without lowering the bar:

**AUTHOR -> VALIDATE -> REVIEW -> PUBLISH -> SERVE -> SIMULATE -> BREAK -> FIX -> IMPROVE.**

The member runtime must progressively increase structured-serving share and drive legacy V4 fallback toward retirement. When CI, deployment, nutrition mapping or visual production waits, independent commissioning lanes continue.
