# Shift member-facing humanness standard

## Product principle

# THE MEMBER MUST NEVER FEEL THE MACHINE

Shift may use sophisticated machinery underneath. Member-facing Grub, Fit and Shift AI must expose warmth, expertise, confidence and simplicity rather than templates, permutations, protocol language, provenance plumbing or mechanically assembled output.

This is a commissioning requirement, not a tone guide. Technically valid content can still fail V1 if it feels machine-made.

## Grub

Food first. The first reaction should be **“Yeah. I’d eat that.”** Nutrition remains a constraint/intelligence layer underneath.

Member recipes must be dish-specific and useful. Methods must appropriately contain heat, timing, sequence, texture, colour, smell, doneness, seasoning and practical cues. A curry must explain that curry; a slow-cooker recipe must use a slow cooker properly; a toastie must explain how to get it crisp and hot; overnight oats must have a real soaking base; fakeaways must teach the technique that makes them a credible alternative.

Voice: confident, knowledgeable, enthusiastic, practical, unpretentious, specific and sensory. Never imitate an individual chef or food personality. Avoid parody “bloke” writing. Direct normal language, occasional useful humour and real-world understanding are enough.

`SHIFT SAYS` may be used sparingly for genuinely useful recipe-level advice. Do not force banter.

Fakeaways/treats receive elevated desirability review. Shift does not moralise food; a genuine treat meal can be the correct recommendation when it fits the wider member context.

### Grub industrial gate

Automated validation must test, at minimum:
- title/dish coherence;
- family-specific method rather than generic interchangeable instructions;
- cooking cues appropriate to the dish;
- culinary combination plausibility;
- absence of obvious generator/naming artefacts;
- slow-cooker settings/sequence where relevant;
- soaking logic where relevant;
- retained nutrition/schema/food-safety validity.

Human second-person review remains mandatory. `schema valid`, `nutrition valid` and `semantic valid` do not equal editorial approval.

## Fit

Fit should feel like a sensible coach has given the member something achievable today, not like an exercise database issued a protocol.

Each canonical movement must quickly answer:
- What am I doing?
- What should it feel like?
- What should it not feel like?
- What do I need?
- How many/how long?
- How much rest?
- What if I cannot do it?
- What is the easier option?
- What is next when this becomes easy?
- Why has Shift picked this for me, where that context helps?

Structured dosage remains machine-readable underneath; member-facing coaching comes first.

Voice: positive, energetic, clear, approachable, encouraging, non-judgemental and competent. Never imitate a named fitness personality. Encouragement should be contextual rather than motivational wallpaper.

### Fit visual standard

Final premium visuals must make the movement easier to understand immediately. A decorative static pose is insufficient where mechanics require sequence. Use a consistent Shift language and, where useful, `START → MOVE → FINISH` or an equivalent multi-state treatment. Equipment, body position and direction of movement must be clear; visuals must remain mobile-readable and carry accurate accessible alt text.

### Fit industrial gate

Automated/domain validation must test, at minimum:
- movement-specific coaching rather than generic prose;
- expected sensation and relevant warning sensation;
- useful regression and progression;
- dosage compatibility with the canonical movement;
- visual sequence contract and alt text;
- equipment consistency;
- inheritance only to genuinely mechanically equivalent descendants.

Human/domain visual acceptance remains mandatory before publication.

## Shift AI

One Shift Brain can use context, memory, provenance, confidence, preferences, Nays, Progress and Knowledge internally. The member should experience a useful conversation that remembers him. Do not surface architecture words unless the member is explicitly using privacy/provenance controls where those details matter.

## Scale rule

Humanness does not authorise hand-writing the catalogue. Repair generators, structured cooking logic, family-specific composers, cuisine/technique libraries and canonical coaching guidance; regenerate at scale. Quality and scale are simultaneous requirements.

## Release rule

Grub/Fit humanness repair runs in parallel with authenticated RC, premium/mobile, accessibility/performance, Dave and quick-kill blocker lanes. It does not pause the V1 war room.
