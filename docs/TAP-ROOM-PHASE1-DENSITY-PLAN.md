# Tap Room Phase 1 - genuine density plan

These are conversation starters, not fabricated member posts. Publish them from Matt's verified founder account, in his own name, only after he has read and accepted them.

## Sport & Banter

1. What result ruined your weekend before it had properly started? Mine usually manages it by half three.
2. Best sporting day you have actually been there for - not one you watched from the sofa?
3. Which player did you swear was useless, only for him to make you look a complete mug?

## Treatment Experiences

1. What surprised you most when you actually started treatment - good, bad or just plain odd? Your own experience only; no dose instructions.
2. What practical thing made treatment days easier for you? Not medical advice - just the ordinary stuff that helped your day.
3. What do you wish somebody had explained in normal English before you began?

## Food & Everyday

1. What meal keeps the whole house happy without turning Tuesday night into a military operation?
2. What is your emergency tea when the day has gone completely sideways?
3. Which supposedly healthy food can you simply not pretend to enjoy?

## Confidence & Setbacks

1. What is one ordinary thing you can do now that felt harder six months ago?
2. Anyone else had a week where the plan disappeared by Monday lunchtime? What helped you get back into it without punishing yourself?
3. What changed first for you: the scales, your clothes, your energy, or how you felt walking into a room?

## Travel & Breaks

1. Best away day or sports trip you have done - and what would you do differently next time?
2. Where have you taken the family that was genuinely worth the money?
3. Has anyone done a solo trip without it feeling like a corporate mindfulness retreat? Where worked?

## General Life

1. What is taking up far too much space in your head this week?
2. What did nobody warn you would become expensive when you became a proper adult?
3. What is one thing you have finally sorted that you had been putting off for months?

## Matt's first six weeks

- Monday: one honest opening post or weekend follow-up; 15 minutes.
- Wednesday: reply to unanswered threads and welcome new contributors; 15 minutes.
- Friday: one lighter sport, food, travel or general-life thread; 15 minutes.
- Sunday evening: read the room, thank useful contributors, flag anything needing moderator attention; 15 minutes.
- First 14 days: no genuine member post waits more than 24 hours for a useful human response.
- Weeks 3-4: reduce prompts where member-led conversation is flowing; keep replying as Matt, not as a brand account.
- Weeks 5-6: retire stale founding prompts and identify the rooms that need a regular host.

Matt does not pretend to be a clinician or therapist. He talks as the founder and as a bloke with lived experience. Treatment questions crossing into advice go to the governed support route.

## Early-host criteria

Invite 3-5 verified members only. A suitable host is already constructive, replies without preaching, can disagree without becoming a weapon, respects treatment boundaries, and is comfortable contributing twice a week for six weeks. No payment, follower-count requirement or invented activity. Each invite is personal and opt-in.
