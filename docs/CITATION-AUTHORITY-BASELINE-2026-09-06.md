# SHIFT citation authority baseline — 6 September 2026

## Executive truth

SHIFT's on-site evidence foundation is materially stronger than its off-site reputation. Public health pages usually expose named authorship, evidence dates, safety boundaries and inspectable sources. Independent web authority is currently close to zero.

That is normal for a brand incorporated in August 2026, but it means search engines and answer engines mostly have SHIFT's own claims about SHIFT. Companies House verifies the legal entity. Social profiles and automated company directories verify existence. They do not establish health authority.

## On-site baseline

- Production sitemap: 257 URLs fetched successfully.
- Audit scope: health articles, comparisons, FAQs, guides, mental-health pages, research pages, calculators and core medicine hubs.
- Automated checks: named authorship, dated evidence review, visible source section, recognised authoritative source, health-information boundary, valid JSON-LD author/creator and modified date.
- Known build correction: add the Shift Some Timber organisation as the truthful `creator` of seven `WebApplication` calculator schemas. The calculators already carry visible UK sources, limitations and review dates.

The audit deliberately recognises the right kind of authority for the claim. Official overseas health-system registries can verify a provider in a country comparison. A manufacturer or trial registry can establish the development status of an investigational medicine. Neither is treated as independent proof that a treatment is effective or suitable.

## Off-site baseline

Searches for the exact brand, domain, founder plus brand, and company name found:

- Companies House records for Shift Some Timber Ltd.
- Automated company-directory mirrors.
- SHIFT's own social profiles and indexed SHIFT pages.
- Unrelated uses of the ordinary phrase “shift some timber”.

No meaningful independent editorial citation, health-sector backlink, earned media reference or third-party citation of SHIFT research was identified in this baseline search. Do not inflate directory listings into an authority claim.

## Best first authority asset

Use `/research/uk-mens-weight-health-statistics` as the first cite-worthy asset, but only after its methodology, source table, definitions, update date and downloadable data are strong enough for a journalist to verify in minutes. The asset should answer a narrow UK question with original synthesis or original anonymised SHIFT data, not merely repackage NHS statistics.

## Earned-authority sequence

1. Make the research asset independently checkable and quotable.
2. Publish a short methodology and downloadable CSV with stable field definitions.
3. Prepare a one-page media brief containing only evidenced findings.
4. Approach a small relevant list: UK men's-health editors, obesity/weight-management journalists, local Macclesfield/Cheshire media, football-community health programmes and credible podcasts.
5. Record earned links and unlinked mentions separately from social posts and directory mirrors.
6. Re-run exact-brand and domain searches monthly; add Search Console link data when access is available.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Bulk paid links, private blog networks and reciprocal-link batches.
- Fake expert reviews or invented clinical sign-off.
- Press-release syndication counted as earned editorial coverage.
- Unsupported “leading”, “trusted” or “authority” claims.
- Publishing member health data before a privacy-safe methodology, aggregation threshold and consent basis exist.
