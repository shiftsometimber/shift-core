# After-treatment support — evidence and boundaries

Date: 7 October 2026
Status: INTERNAL PRODUCT EVIDENCE PACK

## Product rule

The 12-week after-treatment route is a practical starting structure inside My Timber. It is not a clinical service, treatment recommendation, prescribing service or promise to prevent weight regain.

No external clinician review is required as a launch dependency. Public claims must remain within reviewed source evidence and the explicit product boundaries below.

## Evidence basis

- NICE NG246: people stopping weight-loss medicines should be offered support to help maintain weight loss.
- NICE QS212 (2025): people stopping weight-management medicines should receive advice for maintaining changes and support for health and wellbeing. The quality statement describes regular feedback and monitoring, practical action plans, sustainable routines, social support and follow-up as useful components.
- NHS public guidance: people stopping weight-loss medicines should be offered support to help maintain a healthy weight.
- Published research shows weight regain after stopping treatment is common at group level. This does not establish an individual forecast and does not prove that My Timber prevents regain.

## Allowed product claims

- My Timber provides practical support before, during and after treatment.
- The after-treatment route organises food, movement, appetite patterns, Life Back, headspace and everyday routines.
- The 12-week route is a starting structure; support continues afterwards.
- My Timber can measure return and member-reported usefulness.
- My Timber is free and does not require a medicine purchase.
- Men can use My Timber regardless of who prescribed treatment, or without medication.

## Claims not to make

- My Timber prevents weight regain.
- My Timber maintains a stated percentage of weight loss.
- My Timber is clinically reviewed unless a genuine review later happens.
- My Timber tells someone to stop, restart, taper, continue or change dose.
- My Timber replaces a GP, pharmacist, prescriber, dietitian, psychologist or emergency service.
- Food noise is diagnosed by My Timber.
- A 12-week programme is sufficient clinical follow-up.

## Medicine boundary

Stopping, restarting, continuing, tapering and changing dose remain decisions for the appropriate prescriber or healthcare professional. My Timber may help a member organise questions, everyday routines and their own observations, but it does not make medicine decisions.

## Safety boundary

Persistent or worrying symptoms, significant side effects, severe distress around eating, urgent mental-health concerns or medication questions are directed to appropriate healthcare support.

## Success measurement

Primary product proof:
- mature week-4 return;
- answered-step "It helped" rate.

Also track:
- starts;
- first-week useful-step rate;
- feedback coverage;
- support staff time per active member;
- unresolved support requests.

Reporting is aggregate. Do not expose identities, medicine details, symptoms or member free text in provider/employer reporting.

## Release rule

A source or safety boundary change requires evidence review and regression tests. No external clinical sign-off is required merely to publish practical non-prescribing support within these boundaries.
