# Employer pilot — complete preparation draft, not launched
Package H. Prepared 28 September 2026 under the growth brief. Employer funds delivery; member tools remain free. No employer, staff capacity, fee, contract or participant is represented as agreed.

## Proposed bounded scope
One willing employer; voluntary adult cohort, provisionally up to 20 for four weeks, reduced if capacity worksheet requires. This is separate from the founding-group cohort; do not combine denominators.
Provide existing My Timber onboarding, an introduction to practical food/movement/goals, access support through the agreed channel and one optional weekly usability check-in. No individual clinical coaching, monitoring, treatment access or mandatory health disclosure. Participation is independent of employment evaluation.
Deliverables: private participant access instructions, operator schedule, weekly aggregate usage/usefulness report where disclosure rules permit, final workload and renewal assessment. No employer access to member accounts.

## Staffing and private price
Before offer: name delivery owner/backup, confirm staffed hours, onboarding minutes, expected contacts and handling time, fixed preparation/reporting hours. Use capacity formula in operations-and-acquisition.md; weekly delivery demand must fit budget including contingency agreed with operator.
Private fixed fee = agreed delivery/admin cost plus agreed contribution and any applicable costs. Use real cost inputs; no public price, automatic per-head minimum or assumed margin.
Optional home tests only if separately commissioned and approved, at actual agreed cost, with explicit member consent and clinical ownership. Not included in present pilot.

## Privacy proposal for agreement before collection
Employer sees aggregate participation, repeat use, voluntarily reported usefulness and delivery workload only. No identities, diagnoses, medication, weights, free text, individual attendance history or participant-level account linkage.
Proposed minimum report cell: 10 people. Suppress smaller cells and complementary cells that allow subtraction; suppress overlapping/time-sliced breakdowns enabling identification. A threshold alone does not guarantee anonymity: assess context, especially small teams and manager knowledge; withhold report if risk remains.
No department, medicine or demographic breakdown by default. No raw quotes unless separately consented and reviewed for identification. A whole-cohort narrative without sensitive counts is the fallback.
Agree controller/processor responsibilities, access, retention period, deletion/withdrawal handling, incident contact and permitted reporting before invitations. Use existing approved systems; do not store participant identifiers in this repository. Privacy review is a launch dependency, not certified here.

## Evaluation and renewal decision
Before start, record enrolled denominator, first-action definition, prompted/unprompted return, usefulness/coverage, hours budget and complaint/escalation handling. Use four-week meaningful-return definition rather than logins alone.
Renew only for another bounded period if actual usefulness/return, resolved task failures and measured delivery load justify it within real fee/cost terms. No absence-reduction, healthcare-saving or clinical-effectiveness claim.
Stopping rule: stop recruitment for privacy exposure, broken core access/save or insufficient staffed capacity; preserve existing access and resolve affected members appropriately.

## Unsent employer discovery questions
What practical support would employees choose voluntarily? Who owns delivery internally? What size/time period is manageable? What aggregate information is genuinely needed? What privacy constraints apply? Who can agree the fixed delivery fee and renewal criteria? What would cause you not to renew?
No invitation or commercial message sent. Launch requires a willing employer and concrete agreed scope, not merely this document.
