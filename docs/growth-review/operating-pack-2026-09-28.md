# SHIFT growth operating pack

28 September 2026. Prepared for Matt. Internal preparation only: nothing sent, recruited, priced or purchased. Complements the 27 September growth brief; does not replace release gates or claim commercial validation.

## 1. Two bounded acquisition tests

Run only after the ordinary account journey and approved release pass. Matt chooses an existing SHIFT-owned channel; no club contacts, charity data or bought lists. Copy below is a test draft, not approved medicine advertising. Obtain appropriate review before publishing treatment-related messaging.

| Test | Intended audience and proposition | Existing destination | What to observe |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: weight-help entry | People looking for practical help with their weight; explain free My Timber and present treatment availability accurately | Start Here | Can they explain what is free, what is unavailable and the next useful action? Do they reach a saved action rather than just register? |
| B: Continuity entry | People already supported elsewhere, between providers or no longer using treatment; no prescription transfer needed | Clinic gone quiet / provider-switch, then Today | Can they reach useful support without feeling pushed into switching clinic? Do existing members find their saved step? |

Draft A: “Looking for help with your weight? Start with where you are. Free My Timber brings food, movement and your next practical step together. No stock available today.”

Draft B: “Your prescription does not have to move. Free My Timber gives you practical food and movement support, plus a next step you can return to—alongside treatment elsewhere, between providers or after treatment.”

Preparation: select one channel, one message and one destination per test; record posting date, review approval and any edits. Use comparable time windows where possible. Do not run both messages at once to the same tiny audience and call the result a reliable experiment. A small organic sample is directional learning, not evidence of causality.

### Measurement reality check

The existing acquisition implementation retains consented coarse source/medium only, not campaign labels or arbitrary route segments. Recognised pairs include `facebook/social`, `instagram/social`, `linkedin/social` and `email/email`. Do not claim that a custom campaign parameter creates a reportable A/B cohort. Necessary-only choices remain unattributed.

The current acquisition report's “activated” means the existing audited Journey/Passport save definition. It is not automatically equivalent to a useful Next Shift, action completion or weekly return. The Continuity scorecard anchors return to first visible Today; the proposed founding cohort anchors to enrolment. Keep all three definitions separate.

For a bounded, consented learning group, record the participant's chosen entry route separately in the approved coded research record. Do not infer medication use from a URL or send health answers to general analytics. A production route-level acquisition funnel would need a separately reviewed measurement change; it is not silently added here.

Report by test: number invited/reached where reliable; consenting enrolled count; first useful actions; first-step feedback; meaningful returns; assistance; missing answers; staff minutes. Show exact counts and denominators. Do not substitute clicks, login or silence for usefulness. Do not combine anonymous reach with registered-member denominators.

## 2. Commercial worksheet and decision rules

All monetary inputs below are **unknown until evidenced**. Unknown is not zero. Use one consistent revenue period and unit (for example, one fulfilled order) before comparing scenarios. Matt owns commercial decisions; an operator supplies observed workload. No fee, margin or profitability claim is approved by this pack.

| Input | Required evidence | Normal-support case | High-support case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Net income retained by SHIFT, R | Written partner settlement terms; identify deductions already included | Not supplied | Not supplied |
| Remaining fulfilment/partner cost, F | Only costs not already deducted from R | Not supplied | Not supplied |
| Payment cost, P | Actual configured payment terms and transaction mix | Not supplied | Not supplied |
| Support time, M minutes | Timed account/order/escalation work, not a guess about “a few emails” | Not measured | Not measured |
| Loaded support hourly cost, H | Explicit staff/owner-time assumption | Not supplied | Not supplied |
| Variable platform cost, V | Attributable usage under existing authorised services | Not measured | Not measured |
| Other variable delivery cost, O | Evidence and explanation; avoid double-counting F | Not supplied | Not supplied |
| Acquisition cost per fulfilled order, A | Actual attributable test cost divided by fulfilled orders; labour included where chosen | Not measured | Not measured |

Support cost per unit = M × H / 60.

Contribution before acquisition = R − F − P − (M × H / 60) − V − O.

Contribution after acquisition = contribution before acquisition − A.

If R is already net of a listed charge, do not deduct that charge again. Track VAT/tax treatment consistently with the business's accounting advice; this worksheet does not decide it. Explicitly include failures, reversals and unfulfilled orders when relevant to actual settlement terms.

Maximum acquisition cost to break even on this unit = contribution before acquisition, only when all inputs are known and it is positive. This is not permission to spend that amount. Do not use speculative lifetime purchases to rescue a negative first-order case.

Capacity check: deliverable cases per week = available staffed support minutes / observed minutes per case. Do not calculate a number if either input is unknown or the denominator is zero. Reserve capacity for exceptions and time off explicitly before promising response times.

Free-member support has its own cost line and denominator. Do not assume a free member later buys treatment. Organic distribution can have zero media spend while still consuming staff time. Proposed paid support needs its own specific benefit, delivery owner and willingness-to-pay evidence.

Decision gate: proceed to a limited commercial offer only after written responsibilities, settlement terms, payment/fulfilment sequence, exception handling and both support scenarios have been reviewed. A positive normal case with unbounded high-support losses is not validated economics.

## 3. Employer-pilot scope ready for discussion

Proposed shape: one willing employer, four weeks, voluntary adult participants, a cohort cap agreed against available staff time. This is a delivery pilot, not a clinical trial or a promise of reduced absence. No employer has been selected or contacted by this work.

| Item | Proposed scope | Gate before invitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member access | Existing free My Timber, Grub, Fit, Life Back and check-ins | Core account/consent/save/return journeys accepted |
| Funded delivery | One welcome session, clear help route, a bounded weekly check-in with the operator, end-of-pilot review | Name the operator, confirm hours, response expectations and cohort cap |
| Clinical care | Existing clinical boundaries; no employer diagnosis, prescribing or individual monitoring service added | Named clinical responsibility if any clinical offer is separately included |
| Tests | Optional home tests only, at actual cost under an agreed service | Provider, ordering/consent, result ownership and escalation verified first; not included by default |
| Price | Private fixed delivery fee; no assumed per-head minimum; do not charge for non-participants | Matt agrees actual scope and price privately; any free case-study pilot requires explicit approval |
| Employer report | Aggregate participation, repeat use, self-reported usefulness and delivery workload | Privacy review, minimum reporting group and suppression rules agreed before collection |
| Individual privacy | Employer does not receive individual health records, usage trails, answers or identifiable quotations | Agree who can access operational data; document purpose, retention and deletion handling |
| Renewal | Further bounded delivery only if useful, deliverable and wanted | Review participation, member feedback, workload and actual delivery cost—not absence or healthcare savings claims |

Week 0: agree scope, operator, consent approach, privacy arrangements, reporting suppression and escalation routes; verify the ordinary member journey.

Week 1: voluntary invitation through the employer's approved channel; explain what the employer cannot see; welcome participants and observe first actions only with permission.

Weeks 2–3: deliver the agreed support; record staff minutes, issues and prompted versus unprompted use. Do not chase non-participants with individual health questions.

Week 4: obtain optional usefulness feedback, review aggregate repeat use and workload, and make a keep/change/stop recommendation. No named quote without separate permission.

Small cohorts: if an aggregate or subgroup might identify a person, suppress it or combine the reporting period only under the agreed rules. A small pilot is not permission to invent a safe universal threshold. No employer dashboard changes are commissioned by this pack.

## 4. Founding-group run sheet: the new four-week proposal

This is the 20-person/four-week proposal in the 27 September brief. It does not replace or merge results with the older 10–15-person/eight-week protocol. Freeze the chosen design before recruitment.

### Launch checklist

- Matt names the operator and agrees the support-time budget.
- Ordinary signup, accessible inbox verification, security check, login, consent, save, reset and return pass with authorised test accounts. A fictional preview shortcut cannot close this gate.
- Invite 20 consenting adults only through approved opt-in channels after acceptance. No invitations have been sent.
- Explain voluntary participation, withdrawal, data handling, clinical boundaries and separate permission for published stories.
- Define enrolment timestamp and London calendar windows; ensure week-four analysis is not run before each member's window ends.

### Coded observation fields

Participant code; enrolment time; chosen entry route (self-reported); new/returning account; first-action time; reached Next Shift; facilitator help and reason; first-step ID/time; feedback outcome/time; meaningful-action dates; prompted/unprompted; support minutes; withdrawal/missing status; barrier category. Keep any identity key and personal health information outside this repository in the approved operational system.

Targets fixed before starting: 16/20 initial check-in plus Next Shift without facilitator help within seven days; 12/20 first-step feedback within seven days of issue; 10/20 meaningful check-in, recorded completion or feedback in days 22–28 from enrolment. Report all 20 in the enrolled denominator, with not-yet-due, withdrawals and missing responses explicit. Honour deletion choices rather than retaining data simply to preserve a denominator.

Usefulness: helped responses / all submitted usefulness responses, with negative/neutral/not-tried categories, skipped questions and missing feedback separately visible. Also report unique members, not just repeated episodes from one engaged member. The existing scorecard treats “not tried” as an answered episode and “skip” as unanswered: preserve that definition when quoting it and label any cohort-specific alternative.

Ask early leavers: What were you hoping for? What got in the way? What felt useful or unnecessary? Did time, cost pressure, a difficult week or the way support was presented affect use? Do not turn the research into medication advice.

### Decision record

At the end, record each threshold PASS/FAIL/NOT YET DUE; material defects; missing data; assistance; support budget versus actual; top barriers; keep/fix/remove decisions; and next responsible person. Passing is evidence for another bounded cohort, not medical effectiveness or unrestricted rollout.
