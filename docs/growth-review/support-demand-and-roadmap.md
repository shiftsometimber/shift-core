## Neutral interview protocol — prepared, unsent
Show one factual concept at a time, clearly labelled hypothetical. Ask:
1. What current problem, if any, would this solve?
2. What do you use now, and what does that cost in money or effort?
3. Which part would you use; which would you ignore?
4. What human input would you expect, and how often?
5. Would you consider paying for this defined scope? Why or why not?
6. What price range would seem reasonable, too expensive or suspiciously cheap?
7. What would make you stop?
Do not suggest a price before the open answer. “Sounds good” is not willingness to pay. No payment, pre-order, subscription, trial contract or fake checkout.
Log respondent code, concept version, current alternative, requested benefit, expected support, stated willingness/price range, objections and missing answers. Keep actual comments separate from analyst interpretation. Real purchase intent still needs later authorised validation.
