## Company and member decision test

For every proposed change ask:
1. Does this help the member take a useful action, return or find dependable help?
2. Is that what the member reasonably expects from the offer?
3. Does it strengthen acquisition, retention or delivery economics without inventing revenue?
4. Does it improve the connected experience, rather than add another isolated feature a competitor could copy?
5. Can it be delivered without breaking a protected journey or creating an unapproved operating cost?

No feature prevents competition. Consistent delivery, accumulated member learning and trusted relationships are the advantage to earn. Do not describe exclusivity or market superiority as established.

## Economics worksheet — do not fill gaps with invented numbers

Record net retained treatment income (and exactly which partner costs are already deducted), variable fulfilment costs not already deducted, payment fees, staff minutes × hourly cost, variable platform cost, and acquisition cost. Contribution before acquisition = retained income minus remaining variable costs. Contribution after acquisition deducts acquisition cost once. Report free-member support cost separately; do not assume later purchase. Compare a normal support case with a high-support case using actual observed minutes. No recurring cost is authorised.

For this new proposal freeze the 20-person cohort and targets before recruitment:
