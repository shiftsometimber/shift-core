# Shift Some Timber — growth and launch plan

Authority date: 2 September 2026  
Commercial state: pharmacy presentation ready; products out of stock; paid treatment checkout disabled.

## Positioning

Shift wins by being the bloke-readable support layer around weight management: private, practical, judgement-free and useful before, during and after treatment. It should not attempt to win a price war or pretend to be the regulated provider before one is appointed.

## Pre-partner actions

1. Keep the full treatment catalogue visible but out of stock.
2. Use founder, Life Back, My Journey and coming-off content to demonstrate the support proposition.
3. Build the organic baseline through canonical hubs and evidence-led updates, not indiscriminate article production.
4. Prepare partner outreach evidence: working site, product journey, safety gates, analytics plan, financial model and clear integration requirements.
5. Approach diagnostics, nutrition, food and apparel partners only where the offer is genuinely useful and the economics can be evidenced.

## First 12 weeks after provider readiness

| Phase | Weeks | Outcome |
|---|---:|---|
| Commission | 1–2 | Provider catalogue/cost/stock loaded; legal/refund/clinical boundaries signed; live Stripe rehearsal |
| Controlled launch | 3–4 | Small invited cohort; daily operations review; no paid acquisition |
| Prove retention | 5–8 | Measure assessment completion, acceptance, fulfilment, Journey return and support usage |
| Expand carefully | 9–12 | Founder-led organic content, referrals and selected partnerships; spend only against proven unit economics |

## Channel plan

- Founder story and practical short-form content.
- Search demand around treatment choices, tablets, side effects, maintenance and coming off.
- Email only with consent and a useful service cadence.
- Community/Life Back stories only with explicit permission and no manufactured outcomes.
- Partner referrals with transparent attribution and no clinical steering by unqualified staff.

## Decision rules

- Do not pay for acquisition until contribution margin and 90-day retention are evidenced.
- Do not publish medicine claims without an evidence digest and required approval.
- Do not use a launch discount if it makes the first order loss-making after partner, fulfilment, payment, refund and support costs.
- Keep `NEWSHIFT25` HQ-controlled, time/usage/product bounded and reversible.
- Pause acquisition automatically if fulfilment, safety, refund or support service levels fail.
