**Lose it → Live better while losing it → Keep it off → Know what’s under the bonnet → Sort the other stuff**

## 5. Public capability checks completed before outreach

### eMed

Public eMed UK material states it provides primary care for 100,000+ NHS patients and medicated weight-loss services for 50,000+ UK patients. It states it is the clinical and technology provider for SheMed and HeMed, including app-based care, prescription services and medication. This makes eMed a serious candidate for a broad underlying clinical/technology rail rather than just fulfilment.

Source: https://glp1.us.emed.com/uk

### HealthHero

HealthHero publicly offers weight-management packages as meds-only, companion-only, or full support, with organisation-funded, co-pay and discounted self-pay models. It advertises co-branded, fully white-label and HealthHero-branded options. It also publicly lists GP, dietetics/nutrition, blood tests, scans, weight loss, mental health and extended primary care.

Sources:
- https://www.healthhero.com/hubfs/Weight_loss_service.pdf
- https://www.healthhero.com/

### Phlo

Phlo is a strong pharmacy / fulfilment candidate with two registered pharmacies, refrigerated-delivery capability, a live direct-to-consumer clinic and significant dispensing volume. Public material states 89,000+ registered patients, 295,000+ delivered orders, 650,000+ delivered medicines and 14,500+ deliveries per month. The key unknown remains the exact B2B Phlo Connect commercial and formulary scope for SHIFT, including whether ED / hair can sit on the same rail.

Sources:
- https://wearephlo.com/
- https://wearephlo.com/phlo-clinic
- https://wearephlo.com/how-phlo-works

### Thriva

Thriva’s partner proposition is highly aligned to SHIFT MOT: full white-label capability, APIs, data pipelines, clinical-governance options, at-home testing, nationwide reach, clinics / phlebotomy and results delivered via API or branded interface. Public material says 2m+ tests delivered and that the partner keeps the customer relationship.

Source: https://thriva.co/for-partners

### Vitall

Vitall Sync offers white-label diagnostics, custom panels, REST API ordering, webhook updates and JSON / PDF / HTML results. Public material says there is no minimum order quantity and supports branded ordering, checkout, results dashboards and optional branded packaging.

Source: https://vitall.co.uk/sync

### DocTap

DocTap offers CQC-regulated clinical partnerships, 1,000+ tests, a healthcare API, partner-branded / white-label booking, consented results returned to partner products, optional GP interpretation and no minimum patient volume. It is particularly useful as a comparator for professionally collected venous diagnostics and broader GP-led pathways, though clinic collection is London-led unless partner-pharmacy collection is agreed.

Sources:
- https://doctap.co.uk/business/clinical-partnerships/diagnostic-testing
- https://doctap.co.uk/business/healthcare-technology
