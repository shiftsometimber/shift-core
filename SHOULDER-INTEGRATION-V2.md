# Shift Shoulder response integration

Shoulder gear is now intended to influence response generation, not merely label the reply afterwards.

Gears: listen, shoulder, challenge, coach, mate, safety.

The selected gear controls initiative, humour and response posture. Safety always overrides humour. Listen suppresses unsolicited plans. Shoulder offers one proportionate useful move. Challenge is direct without attacking the member. Coach is practical and specific. Mate stays natural and avoids turning ordinary conversation into coaching.
