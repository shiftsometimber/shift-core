## scope-opening

Replace:

> In England, you may qualify for a GLP-1 type weight loss injection on the NHS if your BMI is 35 or more (32.5 or more for some ethnic backgrounds) and you have at least four of five listed weight-related conditions. Access is being phased in, so the group who can get it widens over time. Semaglutide (Wegovy) is only available through a specialist weight management service. This guide explains the rules in plain English and what to do next, whether you qualify or not.

With:

> In England, NHS access to weight-loss injections depends on the medicine, the reason for treatment and the available service. England's current primary-care tirzepatide (Mounjaro) rollout generally requires a BMI of at least 35, adjusted for specified ethnic backgrounds, and at least four of five listed weight-related conditions. Specialist-service routes have different criteria. Semaglutide for weight management under NICE TA875 is provided through specialist services; a separate cardiovascular-risk indication has different rules. This guide explains those distinctions and what to ask next.

## tirzepatide-review

> For tirzepatide, [NICE TA1026](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ta1026/chapter/1-Recommendations) calls for a decision about continuing treatment if less than 5% of starting weight has been lost after 6 months on the highest tolerated dose, taking the person's benefits and risks into account.
