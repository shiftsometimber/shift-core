Is My Timber really free?

Yes. My Timber is free to join and use. You do not need to buy treatment or anything else from SHIFT. Any separately offered products or services have their own prices.

Do I need to buy treatment?

No. You are welcome whether you use treatment through SHIFT, another provider, or no treatment at all. My Timber offers everyday support; medicine decisions stay with your prescribing clinician.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Use My Timber in your browser, add the web app to your home screen, or download the Android app from Google Play. The iPhone App Store version is coming soon.

What happens to my information?

Your saved progress belongs in your signed-in account. Optional health tracking has separate choices. Read our privacy information for details of how your information is used and your rights.
