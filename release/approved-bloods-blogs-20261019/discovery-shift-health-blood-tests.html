BEFORE YOU DECIDE

The useful questions.

How do I choose between the three?

Start with the areas each panel covers and the way the sample is taken. More markers do not automatically mean a more appropriate test. These pages explain the proposed options; they do not provide a personalised clinical recommendation.

What are the two home collection options?

We’re trialling a finger-prick kit and a device placed on your upper arm. The aim is to offer another way to collect your sample at home. A small extra charge is planned for the upper-arm device; the amount and availability are being confirmed.

Can I use either collection option for every test?

Supported tests are still being checked with our specialist testing partner. The upper-arm option is in trial. Availability and supported tests will be confirmed before ordering opens. Complete currently requires a professional venous sample at a clinic or nurse home visit.

Will this tell me everything is fine?

No. A blood test is a snapshot of the markers tested, not a complete assessment of your health. A result inside a reference range does not explain every symptom or rule out every condition.

Do I need a test because I use a GLP-1 medicine?

These panels are being considered as part of a wider support offer. They are not presented as mandatory for everyone using GLP-1 medicines and do not replace your prescriber’s assessment or monitoring advice.

What happens if my sample cannot be analysed?

The final service information will explain repeat-kit arrangements, support, any charges and refunds before ordering opens. Repeat-kit costs and response times will be explained before ordering opens.

Can I order a test now?

Ordering is not open yet. Prices, final panel profiles, collection availability and the agreed reporting and follow-up arrangements still need to be completed before launch.
