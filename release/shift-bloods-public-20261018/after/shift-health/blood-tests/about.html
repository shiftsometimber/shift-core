About SHIFT Bloods.

The planned SHIFT Bloods service. Testing and ordering are not open.

What can I explore?

You can explore the proposed Baseline, Advanced and Complete panels, collection differences and an example of the member journey. Choosing a test or changing an example stage does not place an order.

The proposed testing partner

The proposed service is being developed with Vitall Sync, offered by Healthy Human Labs Ltd. The partnership, configured panels, seller/payment model and clinical responsibilities must be confirmed before the service launches. SHIFT does not operate the laboratory or provide clinical interpretation.

Personal information on these pages

These pages have no questionnaire, payment form, live order registration or blood-results import. It stores only your selected test and collection option in your browser’s session storage so you can move between pages and return to your choice. The collection controls do not send these choices to a testing partner or create a test order.

The website’s existing privacy and cookie information also applies. The Bloods selection controls add no marketing trackers.

Before a real service opens

The final service must explain the organisations involved, purposes and legal basis for using information, what is shared, reporting and clinical-review arrangements, retention, individual rights and the support routes. This information does not replace the final test-specific terms or privacy information.

Final panel profiles, retail prices, kit/collection costs, reporting inclusions, significant-result escalation, repeat samples, cancellation and refund arrangements remain launch decisions.

Existing SHIFT policies

The current website has its own privacy information ↗ and terms ↗. Those documents are linked for context; the test-specific service and data-sharing information will be confirmed before ordering opens.
