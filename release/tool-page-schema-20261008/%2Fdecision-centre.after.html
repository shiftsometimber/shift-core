NHS pathway checker

This separates NICE clinical criteria from current NHS access and rollout. Those are not the same thing.

Local variation still matters.

ICBs and specialist services can have different referral routes, capacity and commissioning arrangements. This tool cannot see your local live formulary or waiting list.

What would different levels of weight loss mean?

These are arithmetic scenarios, not promises.

The tool can accurately calculate future weight and BMI. It cannot promise that a particular percentage loss will remove diabetes, normalise blood pressure or cure sleep apnoea for an individual.
