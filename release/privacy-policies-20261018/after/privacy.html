Privacy

Your health data deserves grown-up handling.

This notice explains how Shift Some Timber Ltd handles information through our website and My Timber. It covers our member, information and support services. A separately provided healthcare or testing service must also explain its own use of information before you use it.

Who is responsible for your information?

Shift Some Timber Ltd, company number 17393135, is the controller for the information we use to run our website and member services. A controller decides why and how information is used. Contact us at hello@shiftsometimber.co.uk, call 0330 311 0981, or use our contact page.

A clinical provider, pharmacy or testing organisation may be a separate controller for its services and records. Working with SHIFT does not make that organisation part of Shift Some Timber Ltd. The service information must identify the organisations involved and explain their responsibilities.

What information do we collect?

  • Account and contact information: your name, email, contact details, account settings, communication preferences and information needed to manage access.
  • Optional health and progress information: measurements such as weight, waist, blood pressure and resting heart rate; activity, sleep, wellbeing and check-in answers; progress photographs; and other information you choose to record in an available feature.
  • Plans and preferences: goals, Grub and Fit choices, saved plans, hydration entries and feedback you choose to save.
  • Messages and requests: information you send when asking for help, making a complaint or exercising your data rights.
  • Technical and accountability records: session and security information, consent choices, privacy requests and the records needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Optional analytics: information about visits and use of the website when you choose to allow analytics.

We receive information directly from you and from your use of the service. Where you connect a supported device or health service, the connection information explains which readings can be imported. If a separate healthcare or testing organisation is to send information to SHIFT, the relevant service notice must explain the source and information involved before that exchange starts.

Health information has additional protection as special-category data. We need an appropriate Article 9 condition as well as an Article 6 basis wherever we process it. A contract or a general agreement to our terms does not, by itself, give permission to use health information.

The table does not extend optional tracking consent to health information in a clinical service, support request or separate AI feature. If we need further health information for a particular service, we must explain that use and its applicable condition before asking for it. Please do not send full medical records, identity documents or test reports through an ordinary support email.

Your choice about optional health tracking

Optional health tracking is separate from basic account access. We ask for an express choice for that feature rather than treating a general account checkbox as health consent. Where photographs or a connected health source need a separate choice, that is explained when you use them.

You can withdraw optional health-tracking consent in My Timber Settings, or contact us for help. We must stop using the information for the purpose covered by the withdrawn consent. Withdrawal does not make earlier lawful use unlawful.

Withdrawal and erasure are different. To remove the supported saved health history, use the health-history erasure control or contact us. Necessary records showing your consent and privacy choices are handled separately.

Sharing with prescribing, pharmacy and testing partners

Using My Timber does not give every SHIFT partner access to your account. If you request a service that needs a partner to receive health information, we must explain, before sharing:

  • the provider’s legal identity, role and privacy information;
  • which information is sent and why it is needed;
  • which prescribing outcome, report or other information may come back to SHIFT;
  • how SHIFT will use any information it receives, and who can access it;
  • the applicable legal grounds, retention arrangements and rights.

For prescribing, the necessary information may include a clinical questionnaire, identity details, measurements and supporting evidence. The prescriber is responsible for the clinical assessment and prescribing decision. SHIFT should receive only the outcome and other information needed for the service explained to you.

For blood testing, necessary order, identity, contact and clinical information may go to the identified testing organisations. Where the agreed service includes results in My Timber, we must explain which report or values return to SHIFT and whether we store them or provide secure access through the provider.

Delivering your chosen service and using its results for additional coaching or personalisation are separate purposes. Permission for one does not automatically cover the other. We do not treat optional My Timber consent as blanket permission for these exchanges.

A provider may have its own legal and professional duties, including medical-record retention. Giving it access to a questionnaire is a disclosure even if the information remains on SHIFT’s systems.

Other organisations that help run SHIFT

We use technology and service suppliers for hosting, storage, security, account messages, support and other necessary operations. Cloudflare supports our infrastructure; Google Analytics supports optional analytics. Payment providers are identified in any available payment journey. Each organisation’s access must be limited to its role.

Some suppliers process information on our instructions. Others, including an independent clinical provider, may have their own controller responsibilities. The relationship must be reflected in the appropriate agreement rather than assuming that every third party is our processor.

We do not sell personal information. We do not give a partner permission to market to you merely because you use a SHIFT service. Marketing choices must be handled separately from service messages and health-data permissions.

Coaching, Ask Timber and automated support

The everyday My Timber coach saves the goals, routine details and feedback you confirm. Its fixed-action coaching does not send that saved coaching snapshot to an AI model. You can correct or erase saved coaching memory.

Ask Timber is a separate question-and-answer feature. Information supplied to that feature must be explained at the point of use; agreeing to optional tracking does not silently authorise a different AI use. Avoid including unnecessary identifying or medical information in a question.

The My Timber coach does not diagnose, prescribe or make a clinical decision. A separate clinical provider remains responsible for its clinical decisions. Any new use involving a significant automated decision must be assessed and explained before it is introduced.

If you send a help request, it enters the separate support process. Removing coaching memory does not also remove that support record.

Cookies, analytics and marketing

Our Cookie Policy explains necessary storage and optional analytics. Optional analytics remain off unless you consent. Advertising storage and marketing cookies remain off under the current website settings. Health readings, questionnaire answers, photographs and report contents are not permitted analytics payloads.

A service update, result notification or password-reset message is different from marketing. We do not treat a support enquiry or a healthcare request as a marketing subscription. Any marketing we send must have the permission or other lawful route required for that channel, with a way to stop it.

Where information is processed

Some technology suppliers may process information outside the UK, including through support access. An international transfer must have a valid legal route, such as UK adequacy regulations or appropriate contractual safeguards with the required assessment. We do not promise that all information stays in the UK simply because SHIFT is a UK business.

Contact us for information about the suppliers and safeguards relevant to your information, including how to obtain a copy of applicable transfer safeguards. A partner service must explain its own arrangements too.

How long do we keep information?

Retention depends on the purpose, the feature, your choices and any legal or security requirement. The rules below distinguish optional progress information from necessary business records and independently held medical records.

When a retention exception applies, we should be able to explain the record, reason and relevant period or criteria. Erasure from an active service is distinct from expiry of protected backup copies. We must not restore erased information into ordinary use without applying the relevant deletion instructions.

Your rights and controls

Depending on the legal basis and circumstances, you can ask for access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability or objection to use of your information. You can withdraw consent where we rely on it, and object to direct marketing.

My Timber Settings provides the available account export, optional health-history erasure, consent and account-deletion controls. An export from SHIFT may not include the complete medical record held by an independent provider. We can explain which organisation holds a record and the appropriate request route.

Submitting an account-deletion request signs you out and sends the request for review. It does not mean every record has already been erased. We will explain the outcome, any applicable exemption and any records that must remain.

You do not have to use special legal wording to make a request. Contact us through the details above. We may check identity proportionately to protect your information. We normally respond within one month; if the law permits an extension or a pause, we will explain what applies.

Security and accountability

We use access restrictions, account protections, security logging and data minimisation to help protect information. Access should be limited to the people and services that need it. No online system can guarantee zero risk.

Our existing health-tracking impact assessment covers a defined non-clinical scope. A new partner, health-data purpose, material transfer or clinical pathway needs its own assessment or an appropriate update before it starts. Read our Privacy Centre and data-governance information.

Questions and complaints

Contact hello@shiftsometimber.co.uk and explain your concern. We will acknowledge a data-protection complaint within 30 days, take appropriate steps to investigate, keep you informed and explain the outcome without undue delay.

You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. Our complaints page explains the SHIFT route.

Changes to this notice

We will keep this notice up to date and explain material changes through an appropriate route. Posting a new notice or your continued use of the website does not, by itself, provide consent for a new health-data purpose.
