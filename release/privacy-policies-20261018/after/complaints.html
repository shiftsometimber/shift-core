Privacy

Tell us what has gone wrong.

Email hello@shiftsometimber.co.uk, call 0330 311 0981, or use our contact page. Explain what happened, when it happened and what you would like us to put right. You do not need special legal wording or a particular form.

Please give us enough information to find the issue, but do not send passwords, payment-card details or a full medical record. If sensitive material is needed, we can arrange the appropriate route.

What happens next?

We will acknowledge the complaint, identify who will handle it, investigate the relevant records and provide a reasoned response. If we need more information or extra time, we will explain why and keep you informed. Where a remedy is appropriate, we will explain what will happen.

Privacy and data-protection complaints

You can complain about collection, access, sharing, accuracy, retention, security, consent or a response to a data-rights request. We will acknowledge a data-protection complaint within 30 days, take appropriate steps to investigate and communicate the outcome without undue delay.

A request to access, correct or erase information is also handled under the relevant data-rights rules. Raising it as a complaint does not replace or reset the applicable rights-request deadline. We may need to verify identity proportionately before disclosing information or making a sensitive change.

You have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office. We encourage you to give us an opportunity to resolve the concern, but our process does not remove that right.

Clinical providers and blood testing

A separate provider may be responsible for a clinical assessment, prescribing decision, sample, laboratory report or clinical follow-up. The service information should identify that provider and its complaints route. Tell us if you are unsure who to contact.

We will explain which parts SHIFT can investigate and which belong to the provider. If a complaint needs to be passed on, we must share only what is necessary and explain the applicable arrangements; a complaint is not permission to circulate your whole account. Each controller remains responsible for the information it controls.

Urgent safety concerns

This complaints route is not an emergency service and does not provide continuous clinical monitoring. For immediate danger call 999. For urgent medical concerns use NHS 111 or the appropriate clinical service. Do not wait for a complaint response before seeking help.

If you are not satisfied

Reply explaining what remains unresolved so that another person can review the response where practical. We will explain any relevant external route without claiming membership of a scheme that does not apply. Your rights to approach a regulator, an applicable ombudsman or a court remain unaffected.

How complaint information is used

We use the information needed to investigate, respond, keep an appropriate record and address service problems. Access and retention must reflect those purposes. See our Privacy Notice for more detail.
