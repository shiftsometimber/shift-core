Your data

Your information. Clear choices.

See what SHIFT uses, understand the difference between account and health information, and find the right control or contact.

Your account

My Timber uses account and contact information, settings and session records to provide access and support. Available export, consent and deletion controls are in My Timber Settings.

Optional health tracking

You choose whether to use optional tracking, check-ins, progress photographs and related features. Withdrawing health-tracking consent stops the use covered by that consent. Erasing saved history is a separate action.

Four different actions

  • Export: obtain the information included in the available SHIFT account export.
  • Withdraw consent: stop a consent-based use; this does not itself erase every saved record.
  • Erase optional health history: remove the supported saved tracking history, subject to applicable legal restrictions.
  • Request account deletion: close the account through the review process. The request signs you out; receipt is not a completed-erasure confirmation.

Healthcare and testing records

A clinical provider or testing organisation may hold a separate record under its own responsibilities. Closing My Timber or clearing a progress chart does not automatically erase that record. A partner service must explain the organisations involved, what is shared in each direction and how to make a request.

Optional My Timber consent does not give a prescribing or blood-testing partner general access to your account. Extra use of a report for coaching or personalisation must be explained separately from delivering the service you requested.

This browser and connected devices

Some preferences may exist only in this browser. Clearing them is different from deleting account-backed information. Likewise, disconnecting a health source stops the connection but is different from erasing readings already imported; use the relevant history or account controls.

Cookies and messages

Use the website’s Cookie choices control to change optional analytics. Necessary storage supports core functions. Advertising storage and marketing cookies remain off under the current settings. Marketing preferences are separate from account, privacy and health-data choices.

Need help?

Email hello@shiftsometimber.co.uk or use Contact. You can ask for access, correction or another applicable data right without using a particular form. We may need a proportionate identity check.
