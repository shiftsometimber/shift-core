  • 200g eggs, without shells
  • 160g mushrooms
  • 160g tomato
  • 40g reduced-fat Cheddar
  • 80g wholemeal bread
  • 10ml olive oil

Method

  1. Use four medium eggs, approximately 200g edible weight, and two slices of bread weighing 80g in total. Wash and slice the mushrooms and tomatoes; grate the cheese. Beat the eggs in a bowl and wash hands after raw eggs.
  2. Heat half the oil in a large non-stick frying pan over medium heat. Cook the mushrooms for 4–5 minutes until softened and their released moisture has evaporated.
  3. Add the remaining oil, then the eggs and tomatoes. Move the set edges gently towards the middle so liquid egg runs into the gaps. Lower the heat, sprinkle over the cheese and cover for 3–5 minutes until the egg is fully set throughout, with no liquid egg remaining.
  4. Meanwhile toast the bread. Divide the fully cooked omelette into two and serve with a slice of toast each. If your pan is small, cook two omelettes and allow extra time.

Equipment: hob, large non-stick frying pan with lid, bowl, fork, spatula, knife, chopping board, grater, toaster, two plates, kitchen scales

Declared allergens: egg, milk, wheat/gluten.
