How these options relate to your answers

For a new weight-loss treatment, initial criteria are generally BMI 30 or above, or 27 to below 30 with a qualifying weight-related condition. Orlistat uses 28 as its lower threshold with associated risk factors. These criteria do not confirm suitability.

Four-week supplies repeat 13 times over 52 weeks. Check the quantity and supply period for each listed pack.

Already on treatment or changing provider?

Have your current medicine, strength, last dose date and prescription or dispensing record ready. Include side effects, treatment gaps and important health changes.

The prescriber reviews your starting BMI, treatment history and evidence before confirming whether treatment can continue and at which dose. Changing medicine or restarting after a gap needs a clinical plan.
