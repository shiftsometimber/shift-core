Already on treatment or changing provider?

You can compare another option without starting your story again. On the treatment page, choose continuing treatment and give your current medicine and dose for review.

  • Have your current medicine, strength and last dose date ready.
  • Bring a prescription, dispensing label or previous order record, plus any side effects or treatment gaps.
  • The new prescriber decides whether the current dose can continue. Changing medicine or restarting after a gap needs a clinical plan.

Compare the same strength, pack quantity and supply period. A starter price is not the ongoing cost. Your existing My Timber account and practical support stay with you.

These are routes to information and support. Clinical suitability, medicine and dose require an appropriate assessment by a prescribing service.
