Shift Programme Benefits

More than access to a treatment.

The Shift Programme is being built around the idea that weight management needs decisions, monitoring, practical support and maintenance — not just a transaction.

Decision support first

Understand lifestyle, medication, NHS, surgery and private routes before choosing what deserves a clinical discussion.

Structured assessment journey

Where medical treatment is pursued, the journey should collect the information needed for safe clinical assessment rather than treating a checkout form as healthcare.

Progress tools

Track weight, waist, blood pressure, habits and other meaningful indicators in the account-backed My Timber member platform. Optional health tracking is available to signed-in members after a separate explicit consent.

Nutrition & protein support

Support adequate nutrition and protein rather than encouraging people to simply eat as little as possible.

Walking, strength & mobility

Movement support designed around realistic starting points and long-term health.

Side-effect support

Clear routes for common side-effect information and escalation to appropriate clinical support.

Maintenance planning

Prepare for maintaining progress, treatment changes, appetite return and relapse risk before they happen.

Evidence updates

Keep major treatment and NHS changes connected to the Shift Programme rather than relying on social-media rumour.

Community without judgement

Support built for ordinary adults who want better health without weight-loss theatre or shame.
