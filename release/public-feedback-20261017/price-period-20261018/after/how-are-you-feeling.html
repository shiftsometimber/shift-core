Why we put “How are you feeling?” first

Weight matters, but it is not the only thing that matters. Energy, confidence, sleep, motivation, stress and how manageable life feels can all shape whether a health plan actually works in the real world. This check-in is here to help you notice those signals before jumping straight to another number on a chart.

It does not diagnose anxiety, depression or any other medical condition. It simply helps you reflect on how things are going and points you towards useful parts of Shift Some Timber.
