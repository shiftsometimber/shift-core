Transparency

How the Decision Centre works.

Written and researched by Matt O’Brien, founder of Shift Some Timber.

The engine is intentionally conservative. It identifies pathways worth learning about; it does not make treatment decisions.

NICE tirzepatide threshold

The engine uses NICE TA1026's core obesity recommendation: initial BMI at least 35 kg/m² plus at least one weight-related comorbidity. For South Asian, Chinese, other Asian, Middle Eastern, Black African and African-Caribbean backgrounds, the BMI threshold is normally reduced by 2.5 kg/m².

Primary-care NHS tirzepatide rollout

NHS England is implementing tirzepatide in primary care in phases. The initial high-priority cohort uses BMI 40 kg/m² or more, adjusted for ethnicity, plus 4 of 5 specified conditions: type 2 diabetes, hypertension, cardiovascular disease, obstructive sleep apnoea and dyslipidaemia. This describes the initial cohort, not a complete current eligibility check. Later phases and local implementation can widen access; check your ICB’s current route.

Specialist weight-management services

Specialist services and local commissioning can use different routes from the initial primary-care cohort. NICE clinical eligibility therefore does not automatically equal immediate GP prescribing.

Bariatric-surgery assessment

The engine uses NICE NG246 referral criteria: BMI 40+ or BMI 35–39.9 with a significant health condition that could improve with weight loss, plus willingness to engage with necessary long-term follow-up. Relevant BMI thresholds are generally reduced by 2.5 kg/m² for the ethnic groups listed above.

NHS Digital Weight Management Programme

The NHS Digital Weight Management Programme in England generally requires age 18+, BMI 30+ (27.5 for some higher-risk ethnic groups), and diabetes, hypertension or both.

Why we say “profile appears consistent”

A webpage cannot confirm contraindications, pregnancy status, medication interactions, psychiatric/eating-disorder context, prior treatment history, local commissioning or specialist judgement. For that reason, the engine never displays “You are eligible” as a final clinical conclusion.

Weight-loss scenarios

Scenario outputs use arithmetic only. The site does not infer that losing 15% guarantees a particular blood-pressure change, diabetes remission or elimination of sleep apnoea.

No hidden recommendation score

The tool does not secretly assign Mounjaro 92/100 and surgery 81/100. Route labels are based on explicit threshold/context rules documented above.
