Medical Travel Decision Guide

How to Verify an Overseas Bariatric Clinic

Written and researched by Matt O’Brien, founder of Shift Some Timber.

Verification should be a sequence: country authority → hospital → surgeon → procedure → outcomes → aftercare → complication policy.

Country regulator

Use an official provider/institution register.

Surgeon

Verify current professional registration and relevant specialty/training.

Hospital

Confirm the actual operating site, not just the marketing company's name.

Procedure volume

Ask how many of your proposed procedure the surgeon performs.

Outcomes

Ask for surgeon/provider outcome data and definitions, not testimonials.

Aftercare

Get duration and named clinicians in writing.

Complications

Get the financial and clinical policy in writing.

Sales separation

Clinical suitability should be decided by clinicians, not by a sales representative.

Use this as due diligence, not reassurance.

A complete-looking checklist does not guarantee a good outcome; it helps expose missing parts of the treatment pathway before money changes hands.
