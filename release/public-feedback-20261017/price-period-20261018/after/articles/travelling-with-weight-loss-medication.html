Travelling with weight-loss medication: sort the boring bits before the airport

Prescription medicine, needles, storage and border rules are manageable when planned early. They become considerably less funny at airport security. Check your prescription, product instructions, airline and destination rules before travelling.

Written and researched by Matt O’Brien. Evidence checked 2026-08-26.

Part of the Shift authority systemWeight-loss injections for men →Weight-loss tablets for men →

Quick takeaways

  • Keep medicine in original labelled packaging.
  • Carry essential medicine and equipment in hand luggage.
  • Check destination-country rules before travel.
  • Confirm storage and time-zone instructions with the treating service.

Documents and destination rules

Carry evidence that the medicine was prescribed to you, such as a copy of the prescription or appropriate letter. Requirements vary by country, and a medicine legal in the UK may be restricted elsewhere.

Check official travel advice and the destination's embassy or competent authority. Do not rely on a forum post from three summers ago.

Airport security and needles

UK guidance allows essential medicines, hypodermic syringes and cooling gel packs in hand luggage, subject to screening and relevant proof requirements. Contact the airline and airport where their rules or cooling arrangements need confirmation.

Keep medicines accessible and protected rather than buried in checked baggage.

Storage

Use the current patient information leaflet for the exact product and strength. Temperature limits and permitted time outside refrigeration differ by medicine and presentation.

Do not freeze products that must not be frozen, place pens directly against ice, or assume a hotel minibar maintains a safe temperature.

Dosing days and time zones

Ask the treating service how to handle a large time-zone change, missed dose or altered travel day. Do not improvise by doubling or compressing doses.

Take enough authorised supply for the trip and allow time to resolve prescription or delivery problems before departure.

Frequently asked questions

Can weight-loss injections go in hand luggage?

UK airport guidance allows essential medicines and hypodermic syringes in hand luggage, but carry appropriate documentation and check airline and destination rules.

Should I put the pen in a hotel minibar?

Only if the temperature is appropriate and reliable for the exact product. Follow the current product instructions and ask the treating service if unsure.
