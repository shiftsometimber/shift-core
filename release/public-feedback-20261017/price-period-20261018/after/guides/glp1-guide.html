The Complete GLP-1 Weight Management Guide for Men

A plain-English UK guide to GLP-1 and related weight-management medicines, including Mounjaro and Wegovy.

The short version

Use recognised health guidance as the framework, then adapt the next sensible action to your own life. A useful plan should improve health without reducing you to a single number.

What GLP-1 treatment is

Medicines such as semaglutide act on the GLP-1 pathway. Tirzepatide acts on GIP and GLP-1 pathways. They can reduce appetite and support weight management in eligible people, but they are prescription medicines rather than over-the-counter slimming products.

For most people, the practical next step is not to change everything. Pick the one action that removes the biggest obstacle in your current week, make it repeatable, then review what happened.

Mounjaro and Wegovy are not the same thing

Mounjaro contains tirzepatide; Wegovy contains semaglutide. They have different active ingredients and dosing schedules. Brand names and diabetes/weight-management indications should not be treated as interchangeable.

Assessment matters

A legitimate prescriber needs an accurate history, current medicines and relevant health information. Suitability is individual, and NHS access is subject to eligibility and local/phased implementation.

Side effects and follow-up

Gastrointestinal effects are common with this class of treatment. Persistent or severe symptoms need clinical advice. Do not speed up a dose because somebody online lost more weight.

Keep muscle in the conversation

Weight loss can include lean tissue. Protein intake, appropriate resistance exercise and a sensible rate of progress can help support strength and muscle.

Stopping is part of the plan too

Appetite and weight can change after treatment stops. Maintenance deserves planning rather than being left until the final dose.

The Shift Some Timber view

Official guidance first. Human context immediately afterwards. We want you to understand the evidence without feeling as though a calculator, label or bad week has summed you up.
