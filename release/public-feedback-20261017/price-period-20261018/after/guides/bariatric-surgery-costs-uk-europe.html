The hidden costs people forget

Time off work

Recovery can cost more than the flight or hospital difference.

Vitamins & blood tests

These can be lifelong after bypass/sleeve procedures.

Revision

Band removal, reflux conversion or late complication treatment can dwarf the original saving.

UK vs Europe: think total cost

Take the quoted procedure price, then add travel, hotel, companion travel, insurance, time off work, aftercare, blood monitoring and a contingency for complications. That is the figure to compare.

What are you actually paying for?

In the UK, part of the premium is proximity, regulated hospital access, easier communication with the original surgical team and often longer aftercare. Overseas, the lower price can still represent excellent clinical value — but only when the aftercare and emergency plan are genuinely robust.

True-cost checklist

  • Operation price
  • Consultation / pre-op assessment
  • Flights / transfers
  • Hotel / companion
  • Insurance covering elective surgery complications
  • Time off work
  • Dietitian / psychology follow-up
  • Vitamins and blood monitoring
  • Emergency readmission / revision
  • Return travel if further surgery is required

Shift Says

Our take

The cheapest surgery is the one that works safely, gives you proper follow-up and does not need rescuing later. Sometimes that will be abroad. Sometimes it will not.
