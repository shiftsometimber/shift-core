Pricing

What costs money — and what doesn't.

Shift's treatment information, comparisons, calculators and decision tools are available without buying medication through Shift.

Information & decision tools

Knowledge, calculators, Treatment Finder and comparison content can be used without a prescription purchase.

Separate

Prescription medication

Shift Some Timber does not prescribe or supply medication and does not take payment for prescription medicine on this website.

Declared

Commercial relationships

If a referral, affiliate or partner relationship can generate revenue for Shift, that relationship must be disclosed where it matters to the decision.

What a regulated treatment price must tell you

If Shift introduces a regulated treatment pathway with a clinical/pharmacy provider, the customer must be able to see the responsible provider, what the price includes, whether medicine is included, follow-up/monitoring arrangements, any tests or additional fees, cancellation rules and continuity arrangements before purchase.

No margin-led recommendation

Comparison and Finder logic must not rank a medicine because it earns Shift more. NHS, lifestyle, medication and surgery routes remain legitimate options where relevant.
