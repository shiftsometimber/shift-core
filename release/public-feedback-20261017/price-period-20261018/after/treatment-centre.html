Treatment through Shift

Treatment information through Shift. Regulated clinical assessment, prescribing and dispensing belong to the appropriately regulated provider, not to Shift Some Timber.

Provider & prescriber

Legal entity, clinical responsibility, professional/regulatory registration and who makes the prescribing decision.

Dispensing pharmacy

Named pharmacy, registration, supply responsibility, delivery model and what happens during stock interruption.

Price & inclusions

Actual monthly and longer-term cost, medication inclusion, delivery, consultations, support, dose changes and any extra charges.

Support & escalation

Service hours, response expectations, side-effect route, urgent escalation, complaints, safeguarding and who owns each hand-off.

Cancellation & continuity

Cooling-off/cancellation position, refunds where applicable, missed deliveries, switching/stopping and continuity if a provider relationship ends.
