TREATMENT ACCESS

YOUR NEXT STEP

From useful guidance to a proper assessment.

Shift helps you choose a route, place the order and complete the information an appropriately qualified prescriber through an appropriately regulated prescribing service needs to decide whether prescription treatment is safe and appropriate for you.

See how it works →Keep comparing options
No instant approvals. No black-box decisions.Our qualified clinician—not an advert or an algorithm—makes the treatment decision.

HOW IT WORKS

One joined-up customer journey. Clear clinical responsibility.

1

Understand

Use Start Here and Shift’s evidence-led comparisons to organise what matters to you.

2

Choose to continue

Review the suggested treatment, browse every available option and choose the route that suits you.

3

Order and payment

Confirm the monthly order, accept the Terms of Sale and pay securely. Payment records the order—it does not guarantee a prescription.

4

Verify your details

Complete our secure checks, including identity, current medicines and the evidence needed to verify treatment history.

5

Clinical decision and dispensing

The appropriately qualified prescriber approves, requests more information or recommends another route. Approved orders then move to prescription and fulfilment.

6

Support and progress

My Timber brings progress, support and the next useful action into the same customer journey.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your clinical order does not open up your whole Shift account.

An appropriately regulated prescribing service only sees the information needed for assessment and care. Community activity, private notes and unrelated member data remain separate.

  • Role-based clinical access
  • Purpose-specific consent
  • Minimum necessary information
  • Secure return access
  • Auditable status updates
  • Clear retention controls
