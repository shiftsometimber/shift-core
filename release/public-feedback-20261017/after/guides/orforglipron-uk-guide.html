Orforglipron has crossed an important line

Foundayo is now an authorised UK prescription medicine.

The MHRA authorised orforglipron under the brand Foundayo on 10 August 2026 for weight management and type 2 diabetes in defined adult populations. It is prescription-only; authorisation does not mean automatic NHS access or that it is suitable for everyone.

What is Orforglipron?

Orforglipron is a small-molecule, non-peptide GLP-1 receptor agonist taken once daily by mouth. Unlike peptide-based oral semaglutide, its design allows it to be taken without the same fasting and water restrictions highlighted in Lilly's US product positioning.

That could make adherence easier for people who want GLP-1 treatment without injections or complicated morning routines.

Why does a straightforward GLP-1 pill matter?

No injection

Removes needle aversion and cold-chain injection logistics.

Daily habit

Some people prefer a daily tablet; others prefer a weekly injection they can forget about.

Manufacturing scale

A small-molecule oral medicine may have different manufacturing and distribution economics from peptide injections.

What did the ATTAIN Phase 3 Shift Programme show?

In ATTAIN-1, adults with obesity or overweight without diabetes taking the highest studied dose lost an average of about 12.4% of body weight at 72 weeks in Lilly's reported efficacy analysis. The Shift Programme also includes people with type 2 diabetes and maintenance studies.

The headline percentage is lower than top-dose figures reported for some injectable medicines, but efficacy is only one part of the decision. Convenience, price, tolerability and willingness to stay on treatment can matter just as much in real life.

Foundayo: what US approval tells us

FDA approval means the treatment has moved beyond “future medicine” status in the United States. Lilly launched Foundayo with once-daily dosing and US direct-to-patient pricing.

For a UK reader, the important change is that Foundayo now has an MHRA-authorised indication and UK product instructions. Access, prescribing suitability, private availability and any future NHS recommendation remain separate questions.

Is Orforglipron approved in the UK?

Yes. The MHRA authorised Foundayo on 10 August 2026 for weight loss and weight maintenance in adults with a BMI of 30 or above, or a BMI from 27 to below 30 with at least one weight-related comorbidity, alongside a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity. It is also authorised for insufficiently controlled type 2 diabetes. Individual suitability requires assessment by a qualified prescriber.

Important:

MHRA authorisation confirms the medicine may be prescribed within its authorised indications. It does not guarantee NHS availability, private stock or individual suitability.

Side effects and safety

Lilly's Phase 3 Shift Programme described a safety and tolerability profile broadly consistent with GLP-1 medicines, with gastrointestinal effects such as nausea, diarrhoea, vomiting and constipation among the important practical issues.

UK prescribing and safety information should be checked against the current MHRA-approved product information and Patient Information Leaflet.

What might it cost in the UK?

Foundayo is now authorised and has begun appearing through UK private providers, but retail prices can change by dose and provider. Shift does not publish a treatment price until its regulated supply and commercial partners are confirmed.

NHS availability is separate from MHRA authorisation and depends on NICE and commissioning decisions. Private prices should be dated, sourced and checked for consultation, delivery and subscription conditions.

Orforglipron vs Mounjaro

Mounjaro generally offers stronger average weight-loss efficacy in its obesity trial Shift Programme but requires weekly injection. Orforglipron's attraction is a convenient oral GLP-1 format. For some people the extra efficacy of an injection may matter more; for others a pill they will actually take consistently may win.

Who might eventually prefer Orforglipron?

  • People who strongly dislike injections.
  • People who prefer a daily medication routine.
  • People for whom storage and travel with injectable medicines is inconvenient.
  • People who want GLP-1 therapy but value simplicity enough to accept potentially lower average weight loss than stronger injectable options.

Shift Says

Our take

Orforglipron could be a big deal because convenience is a clinical feature, not just a marketing feature. A treatment somebody will reliably take can beat a theoretically stronger option they hate using.

The UK authorisation is now confirmed. The next questions are access, price, individual suitability and how a daily tablet compares with weekly injections in real life.

FAQs

Is Orforglipron a pill?

Yes. It is a once-daily oral small-molecule GLP-1 receptor agonist.

Is Orforglipron approved?

Foundayo is authorised in both the US and UK; the MHRA granted UK authorisation on 10 August 2026.

Is Foundayo available in the UK?

Yes. The MHRA authorised Foundayo in the UK on 10 August 2026. It is prescription-only and individual suitability must be assessed.

How much weight did people lose?

ATTAIN-1 reported about 12.4% average weight reduction at the highest dose at 72 weeks.

Does it need injections?

No.

Is it the same as oral Wegovy?

No. Both are oral GLP-1 medicines, but they are different molecules with different pharmacology and product instructions.
