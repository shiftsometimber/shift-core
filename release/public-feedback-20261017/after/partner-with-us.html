Partnerships that make the platform better — not noisier.

We are interested in organisations that can improve access, evidence, clinical quality, community reach or long-term support.

Current partner opportunity

Shift is at the clinical-infrastructure commissioning stage.

Shift Some Timber Ltd (company no. 17393135) has built the public education platform and connected member-intelligence layer. We are now selecting the regulated partners required to deliver clinical assessment, prescribing, dispensing, fulfilment and ongoing clinical governance safely.

We are not looking for a badge or a basic affiliate relationship. The aim is a long-term operating partnership that can support a growing men-focused weight-management service while keeping clinical accountability clear and independent.

Pharmacy / prescribing

Regulated prescribing, dispensing, clinical governance, fulfilment and medicine-supply partnerships. Shift is actively selecting the regulated clinical and pharmacy infrastructure that will sit behind the Shift Programme; we are interested in long-term partners who can integrate safely with a digital, member-first platform rather than simply supply a transactional prescription service.

What we won't build a partnership around

  • Paying for editorial rankings.
  • Suppressing legitimate treatment risks.
  • Presenting sponsorship as independent medical advice.
  • Unregulated prescribing or medicine supply.
  • Fake scarcity, miracle claims or aggressive weight-loss marketing.
Commercial transparency

Relevant financial relationships should be declared. Partnership does not buy control of independent comparison content.
