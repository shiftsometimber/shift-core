Member journey

The member core is live. The regulated-care handoffs are mapped before they launch.

Account, authentication and core My Timber member states exist today. Clinical assessment, prescribing, dispensing and regulated treatment-support states remain future controlled workflows and must not be implied as operational.

1. Invitation

Review Shift Programme, pricing, privacy and eligibility boundaries.

2. Account

Email verification, authentication and consent.

3. Profile

Basic contact/demographic details.

4. Health information

Secure structured health questionnaire.

5. Decision tools

Saved Health MOT, Treatment Finder, comparisons and roadmap.

6. Clinical review

Appropriate clinician reviews relevant information.

7. Treatment/support

If appropriate, treatment, monitoring and support become active.

8. Progress

Track agreed health/progress measures.

9. Reviews

Scheduled check-ins and escalation.

10. Change / stop

Clear pause, stop, transfer or treatment-change pathway.

11. Maintenance

Long-term regain prevention and health support.

12. Exit / rights

Cancellation, export, deletion and retention rules.
