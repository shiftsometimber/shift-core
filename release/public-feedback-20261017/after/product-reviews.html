Product Reviews

Want us to review something? Send it over for consideration - but sending a product does not buy a good review.

We are open to reviewing products that could genuinely make everyday health, movement, confidence or wellbeing easier for ordinary blokes.

What we might review

Clothing, big-and-tall menswear, sportswear, walking shoes, outdoor kit, wearables, scales, fitness equipment, recovery products, kitchen equipment, food and drink, books, apps and other genuinely relevant products.

How we judge a product

Usefulness
Quality
Value
Claims
Would we buy it?

We'll look at what the product actually does, whether the claims are proportionate, whether the price makes sense and whether we would feel comfortable recommending it to somebody we know.

Our rules

  • A free product does not guarantee coverage.
  • Payment does not guarantee a positive review.
  • If something is gifted, sponsored or affiliate-linked, we'll disclose it.
  • We may decline products that are irrelevant, misleading or make irresponsible health claims.
  • Prescription medicines are not consumer review products.

If it's any old shite, it won't make the cut.

That line is intentionally blunt. Trust is the product we cannot replace if we lose it.

Submit a product

Include the product link, price, intended audience and the claims you make for it. Use our contact form to send your enquiry securely; no email app is needed.
