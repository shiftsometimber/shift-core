V3.0H

Prepared does not mean signed off.

This gate deliberately separates what can be built in advance from what requires an accountable clinician, regulated provider, data-protection/security owner or legal/regulatory review.

Current launch status: BLOCKED FOR LIVE CLINICAL SERVICE

The public educational site can exist independently, but live member health-data processing, prescribing/clinical treatment and paid clinical Shift Programme delivery must not be treated as approved until the relevant blockers below are resolved.

BLOCKER

LEGAL-01 · Privacy / UK GDPR

EXTERNAL_SIGNOFF_REQUIRED

Final privacy notice, lawful-basis mapping, Article 9 condition mapping and processor roles reviewed for actual production architecture.

Owner: Data protection lead / solicitor / DPO-equivalent
BLOCKER

LEGAL-02 · DPIA

EXTERNAL_SIGNOFF_REQUIRED

Complete and approve DPIA before processing live member health data.

Owner: Data protection lead
BLOCKER

LEGAL-03 · Data processors

NOT_STARTED_LIVE

Execute DPAs and international-transfer assessment where relevant for hosting, database, email, analytics, clinical and payment suppliers.

Owner: Company / legal
BLOCKER

CLIN-01 · Clinical provider model

DECISION_REQUIRED

Name the legal entity delivering regulated clinical activity and document which regulator(s) apply to that provider model.

Owner: Clinical provider / legal
BLOCKER

CLIN-02 · Clinical leadership

APPOINTMENT_REQUIRED

Appoint accountable clinical lead and define qualifications, scope, escalation and clinical-governance duties.

Owner: Clinical provider
BLOCKER

CLIN-03 · Prescribing / pharmacy

PARTNER_REQUIRED

Regulated prescriber/pharmacy workflow, patient verification, prescribing standards, supply, monitoring and escalation contract confirmed.

Owner: Clinical/pharmacy provider
BLOCKER

CLIN-04 · Emergency / deterioration

SIGNOFF_REQUIRED

Approve red-flag, adverse-event, deterioration and emergency escalation SOPs with clear member-facing routes.

Owner: Clinical lead
BLOCKER

CLIN-05 · Clinical content review

SIGNOFF_REQUIRED

High-risk clinical/treatment pages clinically reviewed with named reviewer, version, date and next-review schedule.

Owner: Clinical lead/editorial governance
BLOCKER

MEDS-01 · POM advertising

LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED

Review Shift Programme CTAs, social templates, medicine guides and paid/organic promotion to ensure educational content is not used to promote POMs to the public.

Owner: Legal/regulatory marketing reviewer
BLOCKER

CONSENT-01 · Consent

PREPARED

Production consent records must be versioned, auditable and separate optional marketing consent from service/privacy notices.

Owner: Product/data protection
BLOCKER

SEC-01 · Security

LIVE_TEST_REQUIRED

Production security review, role/row-level permission tests, authentication review, logging, backups and incident response tested.

Owner: Engineering/security
LAUNCH_REQUIRED

EMAIL-01 · Email

LIVE_TEST_REQUIRED

SPF, DKIM, DMARC, unsubscribe/preferences and transactional vs marketing separation verified.

Owner: Engineering/marketing
COMMERCIAL_LAUNCH_REQUIRED

PAY-01 · Payments

FUTURE

Pricing, cancellation, recurring-payment terms and checkout disclosures reviewed before taking payment.

Owner: Commercial/legal

What V3.0H has completed

Architecture, checklists, content-risk inventory, POM advertising guardrails, DPIA template, clinical-governance framework, incident/escalation framework and explicit ownership/sign-off requirements.

What V3.0H cannot legitimately complete by itself

CQC/provider registration decisions, professional/pharmacy responsibilities, clinical protocols, legal advice, DPIA approval, Article 9 selection, production processor agreements, security testing and final regulated advertising sign-off.
