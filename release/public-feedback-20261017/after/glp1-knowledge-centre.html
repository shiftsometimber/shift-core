What is GLP-1?

GLP-1 is a natural gut hormone released after eating that helps regulate appetite, fullness and blood sugar.

What is GIP?

GIP is another incretin hormone. Tirzepatide acts on both GLP-1 and GIP receptors.

Hormones

Appetite is influenced by multiple hormones and brain pathways rather than willpower alone.

How they work

Modern medicines enhance incretin signalling to reduce hunger and improve satiety.

History

Future therapies include triple agonists and newer oral medicines under development.
