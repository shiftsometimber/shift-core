UK vs Overseas

UK vs Lithuania Bariatric Surgery

Written and researched by Matt O’Brien, founder of Shift Some Timber.

The useful question is not whether one country is “safe” and another “unsafe”. It is whether the specific surgeon, hospital, package and follow-up pathway stand up to verification.

Provider verificationCQC/devolved regulator + GMC/professional checksVerify that the healthcare institution is licensed through the State Health Care Accreditation Agency under the Ministry of Health
TravelNo international medical flightEuropean flight + international treatment travel
Follow-up geographyTreating team may be easier to revisitConfirm minimum two-year specialist bariatric follow-up arrangements and UK handover before travel

Official verification

EU membership does not make private planned surgery automatically covered or equivalent to UK aftercare.

Questions to send the clinic before a deposit

  1. Who is the named surgeon and where can I verify their current registration/specialist status?
  2. Which hospital will actually perform the operation and is it registered for this work?
  3. Which procedure are you recommending and why is it appropriate for my reflux, diabetes, medicines and prior surgery?
  4. What are the surgeon's own leak, bleeding, readmission, reoperation and mortality figures?
  5. How many nights will I stay and when will a clinician decide I am safe to fly?
  6. Who provides specialist bariatric follow-up for at least two years?
  7. Who organises blood tests and acts on deficiencies after I return?
  8. What happens financially and clinically if I have a complication after returning to the UK?
  9. Will I receive my operative note, discharge summary and follow-up plan in English before travel home?
  10. What insurance or guarantee exists, exactly what does it cover, and what are the exclusions?
Do not assume the NHS becomes your routine aftercare package.

The NHS will treat emergencies, but routine specialist bariatric follow-up after privately funded overseas surgery should be arranged as part of the private pathway rather than assumed.
