Transparency

How the Shift Health MOT works.

Written and researched by Matt O’Brien, founder of Shift Some Timber.

The Health MOT is designed to organise information and priorities, not manufacture a clinical diagnosis from a questionnaire.

Why there is no overall health score

A single 0–100 score would imply a level of validated clinical precision this tool does not have. Instead, the MOT separates different domains and labels them Good foundation, Worth reviewing, or Higher priority.

BMI and waist

Adult BMI uses weight kg ÷ height metres². NICE classifies standard adult overweight at BMI 25–29.9 and obesity from 30, with lower BMI thresholds used for several ethnic groups because cardiometabolic risk occurs at lower BMI. Waist-to-height ratio is used as additional central-adiposity context, particularly when BMI is below 35.

Blood pressure

NHS guidance usually considers blood pressure high at 140/90 mmHg or above in a healthcare setting, or 135/85 or above for home readings. A single reading does not diagnose hypertension. Very high readings require proper clinical interpretation, especially if symptoms are present.

Sleep apnoea signal

The MOT flags reported breathing pauses, gasping/choking, loud snoring and significant daytime sleepiness because these are NHS-listed symptoms of sleep apnoea. It does not calculate an apnoea score or diagnose the condition.

Physical activity

Current UK guidance recommends adults work toward at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity a week (or equivalent vigorous activity) plus strengthening activity on at least 2 days. The MOT treats lower activity as an opportunity to build capacity, not as a failure.

Alcohol

The UK low-risk drinking guideline is no more than 14 units per week on a regular basis, spread across 3 or more days if drinking that amount.

Nutrition, stress and emotional eating

These sections use behavioural prompts rather than validated diagnostic scales. Regular loss-of-control eating is escalated because binge-eating disorder is a recognised condition that deserves appropriate assessment rather than stricter dieting.

Weight-loss scenarios

The 5%, 10%, 15%, 20% and 25% table performs simple arithmetic on current weight and BMI. It deliberately does not predict a precise blood-pressure reduction, diabetes remission probability or other individual medical outcome.

Local storage and privacy

The legacy browser Health MOT questionnaire stores its answers and report data in local storage on the device where it runs. It does not upload them to SHIFT or into My Timber. A shared browser can retain sensitive answers after the page closes. Clear this site’s browser data to remove the local copy; that does not erase separate account-backed information. The main Health MOT link now leads to the separate SHIFT Health home blood-test information page. This methodology describes the older questionnaire, not a laboratory service or enabled results integration.

Sources

  • NHS – blood pressure test

  • NHS – alcohol units and guidance

What this tool will never do

It will not diagnose diabetes, hypertension, sleep apnoea, an eating disorder or cardiovascular disease. It will not prescribe Mounjaro, Wegovy or surgery. Those decisions require appropriate clinical assessment.
