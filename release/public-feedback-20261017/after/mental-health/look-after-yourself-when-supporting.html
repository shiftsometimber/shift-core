Need urgent help now?

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to A&E. If you need urgent mental-health help but it is not an emergency, in England call NHS 111 and select the mental-health option or use NHS 111 online where appropriate.

Urgent support across the UK

Supporting somebody through serious mental-health problems can be exhausting, frightening and emotionally heavy.

Do not become the only support

Encourage a wider network and professional involvement. One person cannot safely provide a 24-hour mental-health service.

Talk to somebody yourself

Without breaching unnecessary private details, you can seek support for your own feelings and ask professionals for general guidance.

Boundaries are not abandonment

You can care deeply and still sleep, work, see friends and protect your own health.
