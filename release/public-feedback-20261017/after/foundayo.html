FOUNDAYO / ORFORGLIPRON — CURRENT UK HUB

Foundayo in the UK: the daily GLP-1 pill without a fasting window

Foundayo is the brand name for orforglipron, an MHRA-authorised prescription GLP-1 tablet. It offers a different daily routine from the Wegovy pill, but convenience is only one part of a responsible treatment decision.

Written and researched by Matt O’Brien, founder of Shift Some Timber. Evidence reviewed 2026-08-26.

What changed in August 2026

On 10 August 2026 the MHRA authorised orforglipron, branded Foundayo, for weight management and type 2 diabetes in defined adult populations. The UK became the first country in Europe to authorise it.

That means older pages describing Foundayo as merely experimental or not UK-authorised are now stale. It does not mean automatic NHS access, universal suitability or that every advertised online product is legitimate.

How Foundayo differs from the Wegovy pill

Both are daily GLP-1 tablets, but they contain different active ingredients. Wegovy tablets contain semaglutide and require an overnight fast, a small amount of water and a wait before food or drink. Foundayo contains orforglipron and has a more flexible relationship with food and water.

The easier routine may matter to people who take other morning medicines, work shifts or struggle with fasting instructions. It does not make Foundayo clinically better for every individual.

Doses, evidence and expectations

Foundayo uses a staged dose-escalation route. The exact current strengths and product instructions must be checked against the authorised UK product information rather than copied from an older trial or another country's label.

Trial averages can describe what happened across a study population. They cannot predict an individual's result, and separate Foundayo, Wegovy and Mounjaro trials are not direct head-to-head comparisons.

Side effects and assessment

Get urgent medical help for severe stomach pain that does not go away, especially if it spreads to your back, with or without vomiting. This can indicate pancreatitis. Do not wait for a routine review or company reply. A clinician must assess it; if pancreatitis is confirmed, the medicine must not be restarted.

Read the instructions for your exact product and discuss side effects with your prescriber or pharmacist: Foundayo UK product information. Safety information checked 3 October 2026.

Gastrointestinal effects such as nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea and constipation can occur with GLP-1 medicines. Suitability depends on medical history, current medicines, risks and the prescriber's assessment.

Use a regulated prescribing and pharmacy service. Do not buy products presented as weight-loss medicines through social media, gyms or unregulated sellers.

Price, private availability and NHS access

Private launch prices and provider availability can move rapidly. Any Shift price tracker must display the provider, strength, date checked and what the price includes.

NHS use requires separate assessment through NICE and implementation decisions. 'MHRA-authorised', 'available privately' and 'available through the NHS' must never be treated as interchangeable statements.

Frequently asked questions

What is Foundayo?

Foundayo is the brand name for orforglipron, a prescription-only daily GLP-1 tablet authorised by the MHRA in August 2026 for defined adult populations.

Is Foundayo the same as the Wegovy pill?

No. Foundayo contains orforglipron while Wegovy tablets contain semaglutide. They have different molecules, product instructions and evidence records.

Does Foundayo require the Wegovy fasting routine?

No. Foundayo has a more flexible relationship with food and water, although users must follow the current product information and their prescriber's instructions.
