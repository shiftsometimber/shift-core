WHAT THE ASSESSMENT COVERS

The right information.
In manageable steps.

01

Your details

Check your name, address, date of birth and contact details carried over from your account and screening.

02

Your health

Complete the remaining medical history, medicines and relevant follow-up questions.

03

Your treatment history

Tell the clinical team about any previous medicine, dose or break in treatment.

04

Your GP

Check the practice details and give the permissions needed for safe clinical care.

05

Your evidence

Provide the identity and measurement evidence requested by the prescribing service. Evidence still needs verification.

06

Check and submit

Review the complete record and declarations before sending it for clinical assessment.

Payment is not prescribing approval.

The prescriber may ask for more information or decide that treatment is not suitable. Required clinical checks must be completed before treatment can be prescribed or supplied.
