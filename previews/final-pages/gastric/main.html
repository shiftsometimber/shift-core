Gastric band or
gastric sleeve?

Understand the differences. Prepare for a more useful conversation with your specialist.

Compare the options Questions to ask

Gastric band

Explore the procedure and the questions to take to your surgical team.

How it works

An adjustable band sits around the upper stomach to create a smaller pouch. The stomach is not removed and the intestine is not bypassed. Adjustments are made through a port beneath the skin.

What to discuss

Ask about band adjustments, reflux or difficulty swallowing, and the possibility of the band slipping or needing removal. Discuss the benefits, risks and expected results for your circumstances.

Follow-up and support

Regular band adjustments and ongoing reviews are part of the plan. Ask who provides these, what they cost, and how your diet, supplements and blood tests will be monitored.

Gastric sleeve

Explore the procedure and the questions to take to your surgical team.

How it works

Sleeve gastrectomy removes around 80% of the stomach, leaving a narrower sleeve. This permanently changes the stomach; it does not bypass the intestine.

What to discuss

Ask about the permanent change, your reflux history, and risks such as leaks, bleeding and blood clots. Your specialist can explain how your health and previous treatment affect the decision.

Follow-up and support

Long-term reviews, supplements and blood tests remain important. Ask your team how to follow the eating plan, manage symptoms and get support after the operation.

The procedureAn overview of how each option works.Read the comparison

Gastric band

An adjustable band creates a small pouch without removing the stomach. It can be removed with another operation. Ask about adjustment visits and the possibility of further surgery.

Gastric sleeve

Part of the stomach is removed permanently. Ask how your existing health, including reflux, affects the choice, and what risks and results your surgical team expects.

Eating and recoveryWhat to discuss in the early stages and beyond.Read the comparison

Gastric band

You will follow a staged eating plan and need to eat slowly and chew well. Band adjustments are based on food intake and progress; follow your own team’s instructions.

Gastric sleeve

You will also follow a staged eating plan, with guidance on portions, drinks and supplements. Your team will advise when to move between stages and build up activity.

Ongoing follow-upSupport and long-term considerations.Read the comparison

Gastric band

Plan for band adjustments as well as long-term reviews. Ask who you can contact if you develop reflux, swallowing problems or repeated vomiting.

Gastric sleeve

Plan for lifelong follow-up, including nutritional monitoring and supplements as advised. Ask what support is available if reflux, eating difficulties or weight regain develop.

Questions to take to your specialist
  • Why might this procedure fit my health and circumstances?
  • How does my reflux or previous surgery affect the choice?
  • What benefits, risks and limitations should I expect?
  • Who provides aftercare, adjustments, supplements and blood tests?
  • What is included in the cost, and what might cost extra?
  • Who should I contact if I develop symptoms after surgery?

Ask for a written aftercare plan and time to consider your options.
