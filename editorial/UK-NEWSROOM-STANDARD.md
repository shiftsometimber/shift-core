# SHIFT UK newsroom archive standard

Owner direction, 13 September 2026: quality and trust come before filling the 50+ coverage target. Quantity is not a search-ranking guarantee.

Each article must answer a distinct reader question, identify the UK nation or study population, cite a primary source, separate source/publication dates, and explain the material limit of its evidence. Current access must not be inferred from an old launch. Trial recruitment, policy proposals, service activity and measured outcomes are different claims. Do not invent clinical review, interviews, first-hand experience or qualifications.

Use descriptive titles and summaries, one canonical page, semantic headings, accurate Article metadata and useful internal links. Never backdate SHIFT publication to the original source date. Preserve month-only source precision when that is all the journal supplies. Do not stuff medicine keywords into unrelated articles, promise rankings/backlinks, or split one announcement into several thin pages.
