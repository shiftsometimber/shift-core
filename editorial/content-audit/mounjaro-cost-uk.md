# Mounjaro cost in the UK: compare the whole quote

A price for the first pen is not necessarily the price of continuing treatment. Compare the actual written offer, the service providing it and what happens if the dose, supply or your circumstances change.

## List price and your bill are different things

A list price in a NICE appraisal is part of a particular assessment of NHS use. It is not a retail price cap, a private pharmacy quote or evidence that the NHS will pay for an individual person's treatment. Confidential NHS arrangements and private services may have different costs.

This article does not provide a live price league table. Private quotes change, and an old comparison can become misleading. Ask the provider for a dated quote for the exact medicine, formulation, prescribed dose and length of supply.

## Compare like with like

- Does the quote cover four weeks or a calendar month?
- Are consultation, prescription, needles, delivery and any membership charges included?
- What is the quoted cost at later doses if your prescriber considers a change appropriate?
- Is the advertised amount an introductory offer? What happens when it ends?
- What are the cancellation, refund and failed-delivery terms?
- Who assesses you, who prescribes and which registered pharmacy dispenses?
- How do you obtain clinical advice between appointments, and what is included?

Write the answers alongside each provider's quote. A cheaper headline with extra recurring fees may not be a cheaper continuing service. Do not assume a term such as support means clinical monitoring or a particular response time.

## Do not invent a fixed treatment course

Mounjaro is not automatically a twelve- or eighteen-month course. Suitability, dose, review and continuation depend on individual clinical assessment. A spreadsheet can show what a stated quote would cost over a chosen period, but cannot establish how long you should take the medicine.

If affordability is changing, tell the prescribing service early. Ask about a safe plan and any suitable alternatives. Do not stretch doses, extract medicine from a pen or switch products to make a budget work without clinical advice.

## NHS eligibility is a separate question

NICE's recommendation and the local commissioning pathway are not the same as a promise of immediate access. Ask your GP or relevant service which current pathway applies where you live and what support accompanies it. UK arrangements differ; an England appraisal is not a universal rule for every NHS service.

## Keep a record before paying

Save the quote, the terms, the provider and pharmacy details, the order confirmation and the route for clinical questions. If a provider stops replying, use [Clinic Gone Quiet](https://shiftsometimber.co.uk/clinic-gone-quiet) to organise the next steps. Urgent symptoms need the appropriate healthcare service, not a billing dispute or a company inbox.
