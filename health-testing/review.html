BEFORE YOU DECIDE

The useful questions.

Can I order through SHIFT now?

Ordering is not open yet. You can explore all seven tests here. Heart Health and Male Hormone are also marked coming soon by Newfoundland.

What is the difference between a home and a lab test?

Both start with a sample collected at home. A home self-test is completed and read using the kit. A lab test involves posting your sample for analysis and accessing results through Newfoundland.

Will results appear automatically in My Timber?

Lab results are accessed through the Newfoundland Health Hub. Automatic transfer into My Timber is not available. My Timber remains a place for your own goals, check-ins and progress.

Do I need these tests because I use weight-management medicine?

These tests are optional. Follow the assessment and monitoring advice of your own prescriber or healthcare professional.

Can I compare prices yet?

SHIFT prices and delivery charges will be shown before ordering opens.
