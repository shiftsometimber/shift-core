THE SMALL PRINT, MADE READABLE

Evidence you can inspect.

Our judgement, explained.

“Worth considering” means there is a relevant reason to read more. “Food first / optional” means a supplement may have a role, but it is not the starting point. “Ask a professional” means context or testing matters. “Not a priority” means we would not put it ahead of the basics for this goal.

These are SHIFT editorial judgements, not clinical ratings. The evidence labels describe the type and limits of the support, not a guarantee of benefit.

Who should get individual advice?

If you take medicines, have kidney or liver disease, another health condition, a restricted diet or previous bariatric surgery, ask your pharmacist, GP or registered dietitian. Supplements can interact with medicines and contain overlapping ingredients. Do not use this guide to diagnose or treat a deficiency.

Persistent tiredness, unexplained weight loss or difficulty eating deserve proper assessment. Seek urgent help for severe symptoms. If you use a GLP-1 and feel unwell, have pain or are vomiting, contact your prescriber promptly.
