    How to make it

      01

      Variety beats perfection.

      Include different food groups over the day. Fresh, frozen and tinned fruit and veg can all contribute. Fortified dairy alternatives can be useful; check the label.

      NHS balanced diet ↗
      02

      Give fibre some attention.

      Oats, wholegrains, beans, fruit and veg can contribute fibre. Try variety across meals rather than relying on one food.

      NHS fibre guide ↗
      03

      Eating less? Keep eating well.

      If a GLP-1 reduces your appetite, keep meals regular and include protein. If eating becomes difficult, you feel unwell or have troublesome side effects, contact your prescriber promptly.

      THE BASIS, MADE READABLE

      Useful ideas. Clear limits.

      Food you can work with.

      These are original SHIFT meal ideas built around general food-group guidance. They aren’t personalised prescriptions. We haven’t calculated calories or nutrient totals, promised weight loss or tested these recipes in a kitchen trial.

      When to get individual advice

      If you have a health condition, have had bariatric surgery or need a special diet, speak to your GP or a registered dietitian about what suits you. Persistent low appetite, unexplained weight loss or difficulty eating need assessment.

      If you use a GLP-1 and feel unwell or have troublesome symptoms, contact your prescriber. Keep following any prescribed plan.
