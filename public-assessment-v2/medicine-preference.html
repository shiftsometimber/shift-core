BMI & ELIGIBILITY

Two routes into a clinical review.

30+BMI 30 kg/m² or above

The licensed obesity route. BMI is a starting gate, not an approval guarantee.

OR
27–29.9BMI plus a weight-related condition

For example high blood pressure, abnormal blood fats, sleep apnoea, cardiovascular disease, prediabetes or type 2 diabetes.

Not just a number.A prescriber also reviews your health, medicines, pregnancy plans, previous treatment and whether this route is safe and appropriate.
