Medicine preferences and prescribing

You may record a medicine or dose preference before consultation. This is a request for assessment, not a prescription or a guarantee of supply. The prescribing clinician considers the request with you and decides whether treatment is safe and appropriate. They may ask for more information, recommend an alternative or decline to prescribe.

Please provide complete and accurate information and contact the clinical team promptly if an answer is incorrect or your health changes. Your request may be paused while the team clarifies your information.
