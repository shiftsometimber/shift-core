## Measurement and honest interpretation

Use only supported `utm_source` and `utm_medium` pairs: linkedin/social, facebook/social, instagram/social, x/social, tiktok/social. Swap both values for the destination channel. No participant IDs, health status, medication names, email addresses or free-text campaign fields in links. Do not add utm_campaign: it is not part of the bounded attribution model.

Existing first-party attribution requires explicit SHIFT measurement consent. A source may remain unattributed; do not force tracking to use the service. HQ source totals include other traffic from the same platform; they do not uniquely identify this campaign or prove causal lift. Record each post's own impressions/link clicks from the publishing platform separately, then compare the timing with consented source-level registrations and successful first saves. Do not join individual marketing identities to health records.

Capture a 90-day HQ report before launch and at days 7, 28 and 56. Keep report timestamps and cohort windows; rolling cohorts change membership. Automated returns mean login or audited Journey/Passport writes. Use the separately consented pilot sheet for useful actions, prompted visits, support minutes and post-treatment context.
