Why this belongs in SHIFT.

Getting your life back can mean different things. Feeling able to leave the house. Asking for help. Finding your confidence after an injury. Having a conversation you’ve been putting off.

I want SHIFT to make those first steps easier, and to help people discover the organisations already doing important work. We’re building these connections now, before opening for transactions.
