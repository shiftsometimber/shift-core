## Limits

- Independent AI editorial review; no human, Matt, clinical or professional-dietitian approval is represented.
- Publication remains blocked until the mandatory human second-person acceptance is recorded separately.
- Recipes were not physically cooked or taste-tested. Timings and generic-food nutrient values are estimates.
- Allergen tags are not a guarantee of suitability for an individual allergy; actual bought-product labels govern.
- Approval applies only to the exact listed content hashes and scopes. Any change requires renewed review.
